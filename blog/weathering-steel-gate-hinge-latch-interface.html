Gate Hinges and Latches: Define the Interfaces Before Cutting

A weathering steel gate leaf can be fabricated accurately and still fail to fit the specified hardware.

SHANDONG DAZHI / BUYER GUIDEPublished 30 October 2026

Set the hinge axis

Request hinge drawings showing pin axis, fixing pattern, plate thickness and adjustment range. The axis position determines the swing path and affects the visible gap at the post.

Coordinate the latch package

Treat the latch as a separate interface: record strike location, lock body depth, handle projection and access from both sides. Where a powered lock is planned, show the cable route and service access.

Record hardware responsibility

Decide who provides the hardware and who verifies compatibility with the leaf mass and site duty. Do not infer a load rating from a photograph of a similar gate.

For the inquiry: Attach the approved hardware schedule to the gate drawing and mark any supplier substitution for review before fabrication.

Related reading: prepare a complete RFQ and browse material and processed-part inquiries. This guide is a planning aid; approved project drawings and order documents govern fabrication.
