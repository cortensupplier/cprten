Can Q355NH Replace S355J2W? Build an Evidence-Based Review

Compare the ordered requirements and proposed material evidence before accepting a Q355NH substitution for S355J2W plate.

SHANDONG DAZHI21 November 2026

Q355NH and S355J2W should be reviewed as separate material specifications. A proposed substitution needs a line-by-line comparison with the project requirements and approval from the responsible purchaser and designer. Similar strength labels or a similar rusted appearance cannot establish interchangeability.

Begin with the requirement that actually governs

Collect the ordered standard edition, complete grade, plate thickness, delivery condition and supplementary requirements. Then ask the proposed supplier for the same information for the alternative. Compare actual product requirements rather than two short catalogue descriptions. If a contractual requirement is missing from the proposal, mark it unresolved instead of filling the gap with a typical value from another producer.

Build a comparison with evidence in both columns

Use separate lines for chemistry, thickness-dependent strength, impact requirements, dimensions, delivery condition and required inspection documents. Record the relevant document and clause or table beside each entry. Distinguish a specified minimum or maximum from an actual heat result. A heat can meet a particular numerical limit without the supplied product being certified to every requirement of the original specification.

Identify what a successful comparison still does not settle

Review fabrication procedures, connection assumptions and the intended exposure with the responsible project team. Existing procedures or approvals may name the original material. A substitution decision should state whether those documents remain applicable or require revision. Keep this review separate from a price comparison so a lower offer does not silently approve a different technical basis.

Example of a useful unresolved-item record

Suppose a proposal includes chemistry and tensile results but does not identify the impact-test evidence required by the original order. Record that specific missing evidence, the proposed follow-up and the person authorised to decide. Do not label the entire material equivalent while that item remains open. This example illustrates a review method, not test results from an actual supplied heat.
