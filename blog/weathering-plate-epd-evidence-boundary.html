What Environmental Evidence Should Accompany a Steel Plate Inquiry?

Review the product scope, declared unit and life-cycle boundary of an EPD before using an environmental claim in procurement.

SHANDONG DAZHI21 November 2026

An environmental product declaration provides reported environmental information for a defined product scope. It does not automatically cover every steel grade, processing route or supplier using similar material. Ask for the applicable document and review its boundary before attaching an emissions figure to a plate order.

Identify the document and covered product

Record the declaration identifier, owner, version, validity information, product description and the production scope represented. Compare that scope with the proposed plate source and product route. A declaration for a finished section is not automatically a declaration for the plate used in a different component. Ask the supplier to explain the connection instead of selecting a familiar company name from a document library.

Match the declared unit and reported modules

Check whether figures are stated per kilogram, tonne, area or another declared basis, and identify the included life-cycle modules. A comparison needs a compatible unit, boundary, indicator and methodology. Do not combine a factory-stage figure with a full-life-cycle figure and describe the result as a like-for-like ranking. Retain the underlying document rather than copying an isolated value into a purchasing spreadsheet.

Separate base material from later operations

Cutting, machining, surface treatment, packaging and transport may sit outside a particular material declaration. Establish how the project requires these activities to be reported and avoid assuming they are included. If a supplier cites upstream data, ask how it maps to the proposed source material and whether further processing is accounted for within the stated boundary.

Example of a boundary question to resolve

A buyer receives one figure per tonne for plate production and another per finished component after fabrication. Before comparison, request the component mass basis and the activities included in each figure. Resolve those differences through the project assessment method. This is an evidence-review example, not a declaration that SHANDONG DAZHI or a particular offered plate holds an EPD or a verified emissions performance.
