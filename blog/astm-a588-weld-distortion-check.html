ASTM A588 Welded Plate Assemblies: Distortion and Dimensional Handover

Plan fit-up, welding sequence and final measurements for A588 plate assemblies.

SHANDONG DAZHI9 November 2026

Define the final assembly envelope

The fabrication drawing should state datums, overall dimensions, connection-hole locations and any critical flatness or angular limits. A list of plate blank sizes cannot show the dimensions that matter after welding.

Review the sequence before work

The welding engineer and fabricator should plan fit-up, tack locations, welding sequence and restraints for the approved A588 grade and thickness. The project team approves any change that affects the designed joint. Do not assume a finished assembly will keep every blank dimension unchanged.

Measure after the relevant operation

Inspect geometry after welding and after any permitted straightening or machining. Record the measurement method and temperature-sensitive or clamped conditions if they affect the result. A shop check on a restrained frame may not predict its free-state installation fit.

Keep identification through fabrication

Link each parent plate heat and part mark to the welded assembly. If a component is replaced during fit-up, update the register before closing the job. The mill certificate and the final dimensional report answer different acceptance questions.

Handover to installation

Pack the assembly with its orientation, lifting points and approved drawing revision. Record any accepted deviation and the person who authorized it. The site crew should be able to verify fit without reconstructing the shop's manufacturing history.
