Steel Shim Packs: Specify the Build-Up, Not Just the Total

Describe each shim thickness, footprint and installation position when ordering a weathering steel pack.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Thin flat steel sheets stacked on separators. Photograph for material or application context; component details follow the project drawing.

Two shim packs can reach the same overall thickness while containing different pieces. That difference matters when parts need to be inserted, retained, counted or replaced. Supply a piece schedule for the approved arrangement instead of describing the order only as a total adjustment dimension.

Separate the required gap from the supplied pack

The measured gap is project information; the approved shim arrangement is a component requirement. Have the responsible designer establish the permitted material, footprint, thickness combination and support condition. Do not assume that weathering steel is suitable simply because adjacent exposed components use it. A concealed interface may have a different moisture and protection requirement.

Identify solid and slotted pieces

A solid shim and a split or slotted shim can have the same outside dimensions yet require different installation sequences. Draw the opening direction, hole clearance and any retention feature. Specify whether the pack arrives loose, tied together for transport or assembled by an agreed method. Keep temporary transport ties distinct from permanent retaining details.

Control thickness and edge condition

List each piece thickness and quantity per pack. Define the thickness acceptance basis and any machining needed to achieve it. Include burr removal and contact-face requirements where they affect seating. A cut outline alone does not establish a precision spacer. Where several pieces build up one dimension, review the combined permitted variation rather than checking only individual nominal sizes.

Keep the pack linked to its location

Mark the installation position and pack reference on the schedule. If site measurements lead to a different combination, obtain approval and update the as-installed record. Do not substitute spare thin pieces without checking the permitted arrangement. The remaining inventory should still show which pieces belong to each accepted pack.

Before you release the next stage

  • Order a defined combination of pieces.
  • Confirm suitability for the concealed interface.
  • Record any approved site change.
