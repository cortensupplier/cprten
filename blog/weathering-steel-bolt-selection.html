Specifying Bolted Connections for Weathering Steel

Prepare a fastener schedule covering bolts, nuts, washers, holes, surface conditions and inspection for weathering steel connections.

SHANDONG DAZHI / BUYER RESOURCESUpdated 27 October 20264 min read

Buy the approved fastener assembly

A weathering steel connection should be ordered from an approved connection detail and fastener schedule. The steel plate designation alone does not select a bolt, nut, washer, strength class or installation method. Carry the complete fastener specification from the designer into the quotation and site instructions.

Describe the exposure and the intended appearance of exposed heads. A fastener that looks similar when new may age differently from the plate. Appearance acceptance and structural acceptance are separate decisions and should both be resolved before a repeated assembly is purchased.

Keep bolts, nuts and washers coordinated

List the required bolt dimensions, nut type, washer arrangement and any coating or weathering requirement. Identify whether a special washer or isolation component is part of the detail. Avoid purchasing each item independently without checking that the combination satisfies the governing connection requirements.

If the supplier offers another fastener specification or finish, submit the full assembly for approval. Do not describe an ordinary stainless, galvanized or weathering fastener as an automatic substitute. Review material compatibility, installation instructions and any coating-related limits with the project team.

Put hole details on a controlled drawing

Show hole size, shape, position and datums on the part drawing. Slots, countersinks and movement provisions need explicit details. Distinguish an engineered movement hole from a fabrication clearance, and make clear which face is visible and accessible during tightening.

Check access around adjacent returns, stiffeners and panel edges. A bolt can fit a hole while leaving insufficient room for the intended installation tool or inspection method. A first assembly can expose those conflicts before the full batch is cut or drilled.

Record installation and acceptance requirements

The governing connection specification should define the installation procedure, surface condition, inspection and records. Specify who supplies the fasteners, who installs them and who accepts the completed connection. A photograph of a finished bolt head cannot establish the required installation condition.

Keep package identification and certificates linked to the fastener schedule. At handover, provide the accepted drawing and any required installation records so later replacement work does not use an unreviewed assembly.

Before releasing the order

  • State the governing bolt, nut and washer specifications.
  • Show hole geometry, datums and tool access.
  • Approve proposed fastener or finish substitutions.
  • Retain package identity and required installation records.
