Holding Unresolved Steel Material at Receipt

Keep uncertain material identifiable while the buyer resolves its documents or condition.

SHANDONG DAZHI28 October 2026

Define why a line is on hold

A receiving hold can arise from missing identity, inconsistent records, damage or a disagreement about the ordered condition. Record the specific reason and affected pieces instead of labelling an entire shipment unsuitable without explanation. Keep the purchase requirement visible so the supplier can respond to the actual discrepancy rather than an undefined quality concern.

Prevent accidental use or mixing

Identify the held material in the receiving register and communicate its status to the people releasing work. Arrange storage and marking through the site procedure so held pieces remain distinguishable from accepted stock. Retain the original bundle and heat information. Cutting or redistributing unresolved material can make the later investigation harder, even when the pieces look alike.

Release through a recorded disposition

Name the person authorised to accept documents, request correction or reject the material. Link supplier responses and inspection evidence to the hold record. Record the final decision and any limits on use, then update the material register and notify production. A verbal reassurance should not be the only record supporting the release of a previously disputed item.

A practical record structure

RecordInformation to agree
Hold reasonSpecific discrepancy and requirement
Affected materialBundle, heat and piece identities
Action ownerPerson resolving the issue
Release recordDecision, evidence and date

Before closing the review

  • Identify precisely what is held.
  • Keep it separate in the release process.
  • Retain the final disposition with the material.
