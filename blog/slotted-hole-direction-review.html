Reviewing Slot Direction on Steel Drawings

Make slot orientation and the associated datum explicit before fabrication and assembly.

SHANDONG DAZHI28 October 2026

Show more than slot length and width

A slot has a direction as well as a size. Locate it against named datums and show its intended orientation in a clear view. Mirrored or rotated parts need separate identification where their installed directions differ. A shape copied correctly from CAD can still be installed incorrectly if the part mark and viewing face are ambiguous.

Explain the purpose through the approved detail

A slot may form part of an installation adjustment or a designed movement arrangement. The responsible designer determines its purpose and associated connection requirements. Give the fabricator the accepted hardware and washer detail where relevant. Do not replace a drawing requirement with an improvised slot simply because a round hole does not line up at site.

Check orientation during first-piece review

Use the installed reference direction when reviewing the first component. Check neighbouring returns, fixing access and the relationship between the slot and the support. Record the result on the correct revision. If the orientation changes, review the affected handed pieces and update their marks before repeating the batch or mixing the parts in a packing bundle.

A practical record structure

RecordInformation to agree
GeometrySlot length and width
OrientationDirection against a named datum
HandingSeparate mirrored part identities
AssemblyApproved hardware and access detail

Before closing the review

  • State slot orientation on the drawing.
  • Keep movement decisions with the designer.
  • Review handed pieces before repetition.
