A Deviation Register for Weathering Steel Quotations

Compare a supplier offer against the required specification without losing qualifications in emails.

SHANDONG DAZHI28 October 2026

Record exceptions beside the requirement

A quotation can look complete while qualifying one important requirement in a footnote. Put the requested item and the offered position on the same register line. Include grade, thickness, delivered condition, inspection and processing scope where an exception is proposed. Record an explicit response even when the supplier accepts the original requirement; silence is difficult to distinguish from an overlooked line.

Separate clarification from approval

A question about a drawing is not approval to change it. Give each deviation an identifier and an owner who can resolve it. Ask the responsible buyer or designer to record the decision and any conditions. Do not let a revised price silently establish acceptance of a different material or a reduced inspection scope.

Close the register before release

Carry accepted decisions into the purchase schedule and controlled drawing. Keep superseded offers available for context, but identify the version that governs the order. If an unresolved exception remains, hold the affected line rather than treating an overall order acknowledgement as technical approval. This makes later inspection a comparison with agreed requirements instead of a reconstruction of old conversations.

A practical record structure

RecordInformation to agree
Material designationOffered designation and approval status
Inspection scopeAccepted tests or unresolved exception
Processing allowanceFinished size versus offered blank
Decision recordApprover, date and resulting revision

Before closing the review

  • Assign an identifier to every exception.
  • Obtain an explicit acceptance or rejection.
  • Transfer accepted changes into the order.
