MATERIAL BUYER GUIDE

ASTM A606 Sheet Fixing Layout: Holes, Movement and Drainage

Plan fixing holes and support interfaces for A606 sheet panels before cutting or forming.

SHANDONG DAZHI6 November 2026

Give each panel a reference face

A fixing drawing needs the panel's visible face, top edge and orientation. Dimension hole centres from stable datums after the final forming operation. A symmetrical-looking panel can still have an asymmetric fixing layout when the supports or returns differ.

Define the included hardware

State whether the supplier provides the A606 sheet only, a drilled panel, clips, rails or fasteners. The project designer selects the fastening system and checks loads. Avoid assuming any generic bolt is suitable for the local wetting, salt or coating condition.

Allow the design to move

Panel length, folds and support spacing affect the movement detail. The engineer should approve fixed and sliding points and any slots; the fabricator should receive the approved slot direction and tolerance. A slot drawn without its intended direction can be manufactured correctly yet fail to serve the design.

Control water paths

Show how water leaves horizontal returns, lap joints and the bottom of the panel. Identify materials sensitive to rust-colored runoff and where separation or a drip is needed. The material designation alone cannot keep a damp pocket dry.

Check the first installation set

Compare one finished panel with the support drawing before repeated production. Verify hole positions, handedness, clearances and part marking. Keep the result with the approved revision so site crews do not use an earlier hole schedule.
