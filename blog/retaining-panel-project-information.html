What a Weathering Steel Retaining Panel RFQ Must Include

A photograph of a low steel wall cannot define the panels for another site. Start with the wall design and the component boundary.

SHANDONG DAZHI / BUYER GUIDEPublished 22 October 20262 min read

Original concept illustration, not to scale. The approved drawing defines actual geometry and acceptance.

Describe the retained condition, not just the visible face

The visible height above a path may differ from the height of retained ground or the overall panel dimension. Provide wall sections that show both sides, level changes, embedment or base connections and the accepted support arrangement. The fabrication schedule should reference those sections rather than a single wall-height number.

The responsible engineer must establish ground and loading criteria. State where the engineered design is supplied in the package and identify unresolved items before asking for production pricing.

Draw a boundary around the quotation

A retaining project can include face panels, stiffeners, posts, anchors, foundations, drainage materials and installation. An offer for one part of that list should not be read as a complete wall system. Ask each supplier to state the included components and the interfaces supplied by others.

Assign marks to corners, stepped panels and end returns. Schedule the joint hardware and identify which fixings remain accessible after backfilling. If a change in panel length changes a support detail, return it to the project designer rather than treating it as a simple cutting adjustment.

CheckpointInformation to agree
LevelsVisible height, retained height and overall panel size
SupportsAccepted stiffeners, posts, anchors and foundation interfaces
WaterDrainage and ground-contact treatment specified by design
Commercial scopeComponent package, installation exclusions and delivery sequence

Resolve the ground interface before approving fabrication

Sustained wet contact needs a material-exposure review. Include the specified backing, separation or protective system and the drainage details in the coordinated drawing. A brown weathered finish does not establish durability in every buried or damp condition.

Finish approval and engineering approval are different decisions. Agree the visible sample separately from the material grade and the accepted structural details, then keep all three references in the order register.

Before releasing the order

  • Send the engineered wall plan and sections.
  • Identify each panel and joint type by mark.
  • State soil, surcharge and water criteria through the designer’s documents.
  • Separate panel fabrication from foundations, drainage and installation.

Release component fabrication against the accepted wall package. A product enquiry is not a substitute for the site-specific retaining design.
