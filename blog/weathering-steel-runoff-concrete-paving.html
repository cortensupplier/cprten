Plan where the rust-coloured runoff will go

A sound material selection still needs a route for water leaving the steel, especially during the early weathering period.

SHANDONG DAZHI / BUYER GUIDEPublished 21 October 20262 min read

Map the water path before installation

Rainwater running over exposed weathering steel can carry rust-coloured deposits onto nearby concrete, stone or light paving. The risk is often most visible while the initial patina develops, but a fixed date for “no more staining” should not be promised. Weather, orientation, washing and drainage vary from site to site.

Trace water from every horizontal ledge, panel joint and lower edge. Mark what lies beneath it: a planter, path, facade finish or structural concrete. If runoff would land on a sensitive surface, the designer can consider a drip edge, collection path, sacrificial zone or protective finish appropriate to that project.

Review four interface details

InterfaceDesign question
Panel lower edgeDoes water drip clear or track back onto the support?
Joint and overlapCan water be trapped between close-contact surfaces?
Paving belowIs temporary protection or a catchment detail needed?
Maintenance accessCan drains and debris traps be inspected and cleaned?

Bridge and landscape projects need different drainage layouts, but both benefit from showing the route on a section drawing. Check potential chloride exposure and persistent wetness as separate suitability questions.

Put runoff in the project brief

  • Identify all sensitive adjoining materials on plans and sections.
  • Decide where initial runoff can land safely.
  • Agree temporary construction protection and cleaning responsibility.
  • Show access for drain maintenance after handover.

Use a controlled runoff mockup

A representative mockup can reveal where water leaves an edge, tracks behind a return or reaches adjacent paving. Define the tested assembly, water application, observation points and acceptance criteria before starting. Record the underside and outlet as well as the visible face.

A local test demonstrates the observed flow under those conditions; it does not predict every storm or the full life of the installation. Coordinate temporary paving protection and cleaning responsibility during construction. At handover, identify outlets that must remain clear and record any changes made after the test.
