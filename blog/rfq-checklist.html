How to write a complete weathering steel plate RFQ

A useful inquiry connects the material list with the environment, finish and delivery requirements.

SHANDONG DAZHI / BUYER RESOURCESUpdated 17 September 20263 min read

Start with a controlled material list

Give every material line a reference number. Record the grade, product standard and edition, required delivered condition, thickness, width, length and quantity. Use one unit system consistently. When ordering parts, distinguish the finished dimensions from a stock blank that includes machining or cutting allowance. If you have not selected a grade, say so and explain the application rather than entering a guess.

Attach the right drawing

A panel or shaped blank requires more than its outside dimensions. Include the drawing revision, hole pattern, borders, folded returns, fixing points and acceptance tolerances. Mark any visible face, orientation requirement or edge treatment. Send a controlled drawing for quotation and identify whether it is preliminary or approved for manufacture. Drawings are attached in the email or WhatsApp conversation after you prepare the online summary.

Describe finish and exposure together

Choose between mill finish, natural weathering and a separately agreed pre-weathering or coating process. Include any approved sample reference and the intended viewing conditions. Describe the site environment, especially coastal exposure, deicing salts, persistent moisture, soil contact or sheltered areas. These details help prevent appearance expectations from being mistaken for material acceptance criteria.

Make the commercial basis clear

Add the destination, requested schedule, packing requirements and preferred delivery basis with its named place or port. Identify the required inspection records before quotation. The website RFQ creates a structured summary in your browser. Review it, copy or download it if needed, then send it to cortensupplier@gmail.com or through WhatsApp. Generating the summary does not transmit it or create a purchase order.

Attach a controlled drawing package

Include a readable dimensioned drawing, a part or size schedule, any necessary CAD file, the finish reference and the inspection requirements. Name the revision and tell the supplier whether the files are for quotation or approved production. A new file in an email should not silently replace the accepted revision.

For a small-quantity inquiry, state the actual number of pieces and whether cutting from a parent plate is acceptable. Ask how minimum processing charges, remaining material, certificates and packing are included. Compare the total delivered scope instead of assuming a per-tonne price describes the cost of a few finished parts.
