Mill Edge vs Cut Edge: Define the Delivered Plate Width

State whether quoted weathering plate dimensions refer to mill edges, trimmed edges or a finished cut blank.

SHANDONG DAZHI23 November 2026

Define the supplied edge condition and the stage at which plate width is measured. Mill-edge stock, trimmed plate and a finished cut blank can represent different supply scopes even when their nominal widths look similar on a quotation. The order should state the usable outline it actually requires.

Name the edges that belong to the supplied product

Show which sides retain their producer-supplied condition and which sides will be cut or machined. Include the end treatment if it matters to the next operation. A note requesting cut to length does not automatically define the treatment of both long sides. Confirm whether the quotation describes stock, a prepared blank or the final component.

Separate nominal stock size from the required outline

For a part that needs later machining or trimming, provide the delivered blank drawing and the intended finished outline. State the agreed allowance rather than assuming that the nominal stock width contains enough usable material everywhere. The processor can review the available plate against the required geometry before allocating it to the order.

Put dimensional acceptance on the correct stage

Specify the relevant width, length, squareness and edge-condition requirements for the delivered item. Use the applicable product requirements for stock and the accepted fabrication drawing for processed blanks. Do not expect a raw edge to meet a finished machined-face requirement unless that processing is included. Identify any features from which dimensions are to be taken.

Compare quotations using the same edge scope

List trimming, cutting, machining and edge finishing as named inclusions or exclusions. If one offer supplies stock with remaining edge preparation and another supplies a ready-to-use outline, review the extra material and operations required by each. Agree how inspection will confirm the finished width before describing the two offers as commercially equivalent.
