Steel Plate Certificate Verification: Check the Issuer and Revision

Verify inspection-record identity and document history through a trusted issuer route when a certificate copy raises questions.

SHANDONG DAZHI23 November 2026

A certificate PDF should be checked as a document as well as a material record. Confirm the issuer, report identity, revision and link to the delivered plate. A logo, scan, QR code or supplier stamp alone is not a complete verification of authenticity or applicability.

Preserve the received version

Keep the original file, its filename, date received and sending party with the purchase record. Record the report number and any visible issue or revision information. Avoid editing the document to combine pages or add annotations over original results. Store review notes separately so another person can see precisely which version was assessed.

Use an independently established verification route

When the document is in doubt, contact the claimed issuer through an already trusted business route or independently verified official contact. Do not rely only on a new contact printed inside the disputed file. Share the relevant report identifiers and ask what the issuer can confirm under its document-verification process. Restrict any sensitive order information to the authorised parties.

Ask what a revised record changes

Request an explanation of the changed fields, the reason for revision and the status of the earlier issue. An updated address is a different matter from an amended heat number or test result. Have the responsible quality reviewer consider whether the change affects the acceptance decision. Keep the earlier file available with its superseded or disputed status clearly identified.

Verify applicability separately from authenticity

Even an authentic report may describe a different heat, product, size or delivery condition. Reconcile the document with the plate marks and controlled cut-piece records. If identification is missing, resolve the physical-material connection rather than treating issuer confirmation as proof of the entire delivery. Record both the authenticity response and the material applicability review before closing the question.

Two distinct verification questions

Review itemBasis or example
Document identityIssuer, report number, issue and revision
Issuer responseTrusted verification route and confirmed scope
Material connectionHeat, plate or part identification
DispositionOpen questions and responsible review decision

Confirm who issued the record and which supplied material it actually represents.
