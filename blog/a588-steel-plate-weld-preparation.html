A588 Steel Plate Weld Preparation: What the Buyer Must Specify

Define joint preparation, grade, service conditions and approval responsibilities before ordering welded A588 parts.

SHANDONG DAZHI2 November 2026

Define joint preparation, grade, service conditions and approval responsibilities before ordering welded A588 parts.

The material grade does not define the joint

A588/A588M covers structural steel used in welded construction, but it does not replace an approved welding design. Send the actual grade, thickness range, joint drawing and intended service to the responsible welding engineer. Ask the fabricator to confirm the procedure and qualification basis for the proposed work.

Describe the delivered edge

Specify whether the supplier provides a square-cut plate, a bevel, a root face or a fully fabricated joint. Put bevel angle, root opening and inspection datums on the drawing where they are required. The phrase “ready for welding” is too broad for a price or acceptance test.

Consider exposed weld appearance

If the assembly will remain unpainted, discuss the appearance and corrosion behavior expected at the weld area with the project engineer. Do not assume a plate’s weathering designation determines filler choice or guarantees uniform color. Approve a representative fabrication sample if visible appearance matters.

Make inspection a separate line

List any required visual, dimensional or nondestructive examination and who approves the reports. Link repaired areas to the same drawing and part identity. A quoted plate price should not silently include or exclude fabrication inspection.
