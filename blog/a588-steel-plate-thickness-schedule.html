A588 Steel Plate Thickness Schedule for Multi-Part Orders

Build a thickness and size schedule that keeps A588 plate blanks, purchased plate and finished dimensions separate.

SHANDONG DAZHI2 November 2026

Build a thickness and size schedule that keeps A588 plate blanks, purchased plate and finished dimensions separate.

Three dimensions can describe different things

A mill plate thickness, a nominal drawing thickness and a measured finished component are not automatically interchangeable. For each part, say which dimension the supplier must quote and which dimension the fabricator must inspect. Include the governing drawing revision and the applicable purchase standard.

Group by procurement need

Make one row per grade, nominal thickness, ordered plate size and required quantity. Put cut blanks on a separate schedule with their final length and width, kerf allowance and edge condition. This stops a supplier from quoting one large plate where the buyer expected individually identified pieces.

Record fabrication constraints

A narrow strip, a part with close holes or a large weld preparation may have more waste or handling work than a plain rectangle. Attach the cutting drawing and specify whether remnants can be reused. Ask for a nesting proposal only after the material grade and heat-traceability rules are agreed.

Check the delivered list

At receipt, compare plate marks, piece counts and measured dimensions with the schedule. An approved substitution in one thickness should appear on a revised line item. Do not treat a packing list as proof that every cut part has the specified finished dimension.
