Bend Reliefs Where Two Weathering Steel Folds Meet

Resolve corner relief geometry and the intended corner finish before a folded panel is cut.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Flat steel sheets stored in a workshop. Photograph for material or application context; component details follow the project drawing.

When two folded edges meet, the corner needs a deliberate detail. A relief opening, an overlapping return and a welded corner produce different fabrication tasks and visible results. Show the intended corner condition instead of expecting the flat pattern to decide it automatically.

Draw the finished corner first

Use a section or enlarged view to explain which faces meet and whether the corner remains open, overlaps or receives a weld. Include the inside and outside appearance where both can be seen. The flat development should follow this accepted geometry. A closed line in a CAD outline does not tell the fabricator whether a small opening is intentional.

Review the relief against the actual forming route

Ask the processor to review the proposed relief with the material thickness, bend radii and tool arrangement. Do not copy a relief size from a different sheet thickness without checking it. If the processor proposes a change, obtain approval for its effect on the finished opening and nearby features. The relevant decision is the acceptable component, not simply whether the machine can bend the blank.

Separate appearance from water management

An open corner may be visible and may also connect to a drainage route. A closed or welded corner needs its own project detail. Ask the designer to resolve these functions together rather than closing a gap solely to improve a photograph. State which surfaces need further finishing after forming or welding, and include any accessible sharp edges in the inspection scope.

Use a corner sample for repeated panels

For a large repeated run, a small sample can show the agreed relief, tool marks and corner finish more clearly than a perspective rendering. Identify the material and drawing revision represented by that sample. Keep the approved corner detail attached to the production package so later batches do not revert to an earlier flat pattern.
