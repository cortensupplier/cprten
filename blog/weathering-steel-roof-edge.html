Weathering Steel Roof Edges and Flashing Interfaces

Coordinate weathering steel roof-edge pieces with waterproofing, laps, outlets, movement and stain-sensitive adjoining finishes.

SHANDONG DAZHI / BUYER RESOURCESUpdated 27 October 20264 min read

Define the boundary between steelwork and waterproofing

A weathering steel edge or flashing piece is one component of a roof assembly. Identify which contractor designs the roof waterproofing, which supplies the steel and who approves the interface. A folded steel profile should not be treated as a complete waterproofing specification.

Provide sections at the roof perimeter, corners, joints and penetrations. Show the adjoining membrane, upstands, supports and intended outlets. Define visible-face expectations and the distinction between a decorative edge and a component relied on by the roof designer for water management.

Trace water to an accepted outlet

Review the route of water over the exposed steel and underneath the profile. State where rust-coloured runoff can discharge without staining sensitive finishes or entering the building envelope. Check whether folded returns or lap joints can hold water against the underside.

Coordinate drip details, gutter interfaces and maintenance access with the roof designer. Any mockup or water test should follow an agreed procedure and acceptance basis. A small sample wetting test can show a local flow path but does not certify the performance of an entire roof in service.

Coordinate joints, movement and fixings

Long runs need an approved joint and movement arrangement. Identify the fixed and sliding points, piece lengths, lap directions and corner transitions on the production drawings. Avoid a detail that locks movement at one joint while an adjacent component is intended to slide.

Check fastener access and compatibility with the support and adjoining metals. Identify seals, gaskets and coatings through the approved assembly specification. Do not assume a particular sealant or primer is compatible with an established weathered surface or the roof membrane.

Document fabrication and site acceptance

Include the profile dimensions, inside or outside bend references, material designation, finish, handed pieces and quantities in the order. Mark which dimensions depend on confirmed site measurements and who releases the final schedule for manufacture.

At handover, record joint locations, outlet access, accepted mockup references and any cleaning or inspection instructions. Keep the roof contractor acceptance and the steelwork inspection records connected so future maintenance can understand the interface.

Before releasing the order

  • Resolve roof-design and steelwork responsibilities.
  • Draw the complete water path and outlet.
  • Approve joints, movement, supports and fasteners.
  • Release measured profiles and retain handover records.
