Storing Weathering Steel Plate Before Installation

A storage checklist for weathering steel plate: supports, ventilation, moisture checks, identity and port handover records.

SHANDONG DAZHI / BUYER RESOURCESUpdated 27 October 20264 min read

Keep stored plate supported and able to dry

Weathering steel plate should arrive with an agreed storage and handling plan. A material intended to weather outdoors can still suffer avoidable staining, trapped moisture and handling damage while stacked. Storage is a temporary condition with different contact surfaces and drainage paths from the finished structure.

Choose a stable, drained storage position and keep the plate clear of soil and standing water. Agree the support layout with the supplier and handling contractor so the stack remains stable and the plate is supported without creating unintended distortion. Do not use a visible architectural face as an unprotected bearing surface.

Inspect separators and covers

Place compatible separators at the agreed positions and keep them free of contamination. A wet separator can hold moisture against one small area for an extended period. Check the contact points when plates are moved rather than judging the entire stack from its exposed top surface.

If a cover is used, arrange it to shed rain while allowing the stored material to ventilate. Tight wrapping around wet plate can retain moisture. Inspect under the cover after rain and record whether water has entered, pooled or remained between surfaces. Replace damaged packaging through an agreed handling method.

Treat port storage as a separate handover

A shipment may be unloaded, inspected, stored and reloaded before reaching the fabricator. Identify who checks packaging at each transfer and who reports delays or wet cargo. For coastal or port waiting areas, tell the logistics provider about salt spray, open storage and the expected duration.

Keep the bundle number, heat identity and packing list connected when material is split or re-stacked. Photograph the bundle labels, supports and any damage at receipt. Condition photos complement the material documents; they do not prove a grade or establish that internal surfaces stayed dry.

Release material to fabrication with records

Before cutting, record visible condition on both faces where accessible. Separate mill scale, ordinary surface variation, impact damage and suspected persistent wet-contact areas in the inspection record. Refer acceptance questions to the agreed specification instead of promising that every mark will disappear during weathering.

A useful storage register names the location, bundle identity, inspection date, cover condition and action taken. Where a pre-weathered finish is ordered, follow the approved handling instructions and protect the sample reference used for appearance acceptance.

Before releasing the order

  • Agree the storage location and support arrangement before delivery.
  • Check trapped moisture at separators and under covers.
  • Retain bundle and heat identity after every transfer.
  • Record condition before fabrication and resolve discrepancies.
