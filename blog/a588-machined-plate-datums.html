Machined ASTM A588 Plate: Datums, Allowances and Handover

Plan A588 plate machining from rough blank to finished part with controlled datums and heat identity.

SHANDONG DAZHI5 November 2026

Separate blank from finished size

A machined part needs a rough blank allowance, a finished drawing and an inspection plan. State which dimensions are controlled after machining and which are inherited from the mill plate. The grade and heat identity should follow the blank.

Choose stable datums

Define the face and edges used for setup, hole location and final measurement. If the plate has a visible weathering face, mark it before machining. A drawing that dimensions every feature from different rough edges can create avoidable disagreement at inspection.

Clarify surface treatment

Ask whether cut edges, machined faces and exposed holes receive additional finishing. Machining may remove an existing surface appearance; the accepted delivery condition should describe the finished part rather than the starting plate.

Close the records

Record finished dimensions, piece marks, certificate link and any rework against the drawing revision. Package machined surfaces to prevent transit damage. A quoted machining service should not be mistaken for an assurance of structural fit without the approved tolerance basis.

Inspection datum example

A large connection plate may be cut with extra stock on two edges, then machined from a central datum. Show which surfaces are sacrificial allowance and which remain as weathering faces. This prevents the machine shop from removing the intended visible face while achieving nominal size.
