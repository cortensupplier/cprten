ASTM A588 Plate Export Packing: Keep Marks, Edges and Documents Together

A receiving-focused export packing plan for A588 plate blanks that protects edges and keeps member IDs traceable.

SHANDONG DAZHI10 November 2026

Work backwards from unloading

Plate can leave a cutting shop correctly identified and arrive as an undifferentiated stack. The packing plan should start with the receiver's unloading method, lifting capacity and order of use. Ask the supplier to group blanks by girder or work package where practical, then list every piece mark in the package manifest.

Support the actual geometry

Thin, long or irregular A588 blanks need a different support pattern from a compact rectangle. Specify dunnage location, edge protection and tie-down method according to the approved packing design. Prevent metal banding from obscuring the only surviving material mark. Do not assume that surface patina or a handwritten bundle label proves grade.

Protect the record chain

Each bundle should be tied to the packing list, drawing revision and relevant heat or batch IDs. If a plate is divided after receipt, the receiving team needs the transfer-mark procedure before the first cut. Ask for electronic copies of the agreed material records ahead of the shipment so mismatches can be resolved while packages are still intact.

Define moisture expectations

Weathering steel is intended for suitable exposure, but enclosed wet packaging can cause uneven staining and complicate inspection. Agree whether surfaces must be dry at packing, how condensation is managed and what condition is acceptable at arrival. A cosmetic finish requirement should be written separately from material identity.

Make the receiving checklist short

Check package count, visible damage, marks, dimensions and document references before dispersing the load. Photograph discrepancies and quarantine uncertain pieces.
