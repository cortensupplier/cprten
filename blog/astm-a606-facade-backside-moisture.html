ASTM A606 Facade Sheet: Backside Moisture and Drainage Review

A design handover guide for A606 sheet where the visible face weathers differently from the sheltered back.

SHANDONG DAZHI7 November 2026

Map both sides of the panel

An exposed front and a sheltered back do not experience the same wetting and drying cycle. Show the cavity, support rails, insulation interface and any wrap or membrane on a section drawing. A material type cannot substitute for a drainable wall detail.

Find trapped water

Inspect bottom returns, folded pockets, overlap joints and horizontal ledges. Indicate where water enters, leaves and may be retained. If runoff can stain stone, concrete or glazing, the designer should detail collection or a deliberate drip path.

Coordinate attachment materials

Fasteners, clips and isolators may sit in a different moisture condition from the front face. Specify the approved hardware system and access for later inspection. Keep movement slots and fixing points on a controlled drawing, not in a product photo.

Define finish zones

State whether the backside is bare, coated or otherwise protected, and when any coating is applied relative to forming and drilling. The project designer and coatings specialist should approve the system for the actual cavity conditions.

Make inspection possible

Give installers a checklist for drain openings, cavity clearance and panel orientation before closing the wall. Record deviations and as-built changes, because a blocked drain can defeat an otherwise suitable material selection.

Send the right information

See the related product inquiry and product directory, or send the approved specification and drawing for a scope review.
