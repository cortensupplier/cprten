Before Steel Delivery: Confirm the Appointment and Unloading Route

Give the receiving team usable package information and confirm who coordinates arrival and unloading.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Flat steel plates stacked on a transport bed. Photograph for material or application context; component details follow the project drawing.

A steel delivery needs a receiving plan as well as a street address. Confirm the arrival arrangement, package information and unloading responsibility before the vehicle is dispatched. Clear coordination reduces avoidable waiting and prevents the receiving team from planning around incomplete load information.

Name a receiving coordinator

Provide the person who can confirm the delivery slot, entry point and local instructions. Check whether the site requires an appointment, advance vehicle details or a designated holding area. Keep the coordinator informed if the dispatch date changes. A telephone number on the purchase order is useful only if it reaches someone who can act during the actual arrival window.

Send the real package schedule

Share package dimensions, masses, quantities and any agreed handling restrictions. Identify the heaviest or largest piece and any projections that affect access. Provide the final information when it becomes available rather than leaving the receiving team with a preliminary estimate. The responsible unloading parties must confirm that the proposed equipment and handling arrangement suit the delivered load.

Describe the route after the gate

Review the permitted unloading area and the path to the temporary storage position. Consider whether packages must be received in a particular order because one bundle blocks another on the vehicle. Any required handling or restraint sequence should come from the competent parties responsible for that operation, using the actual load arrangement.

Connect arrival to receiving records

Have the current packing list and order references available at the delivery point. Record receipt and visible discrepancies by package identity before the documents become separated from the load. Where a package cannot be checked immediately, note the inspection status clearly. A signed delivery receipt and a completed technical acceptance check serve different purposes in the receiving process.

Pre-arrival information exchange

ItemInformation to define
AppointmentSlot, gate and receiving coordinator
PackagesDimensions, mass and identities
UnloadingResponsible parties and confirmed arrangement
ReceiptPacking list and inspection status

Before you release the next stage

  • Confirm arrival details before dispatch.
  • Send the actual package schedule.
  • Make receiving records available where the load arrives.
