Grinding a Plate Surface: Check the Repair and Remaining Thickness

Document surface observations and obtain an accepted repair scope before grinding weathering steel plate for appearance or defect removal.

SHANDONG DAZHI23 November 2026

Grinding removes material, so a proposed surface repair needs review against the ordered plate and component requirements. Record the original observation, the authorised repair route and the required checks afterward. An improved-looking surface alone cannot establish that the repaired plate remains acceptable.

Record the condition before altering it

Identify the plate or part, observation location and processing stage. Take suitably referenced photographs and retain any relevant measurements. Describe what was observed without declaring a metallurgical cause from appearance alone. Cleaning for examination and grinding away material are different actions; agree the permitted investigation before the original evidence is lost.

Establish who can approve the proposed repair

Review the applicable product specification, surface requirements and project drawing. Ask the responsible quality or engineering party to determine whether the proposed action is allowed, what limitations apply and whether producer involvement is required. Do not copy a repair allowance from another steel grade or product form. Any welding repair is a separate proposal with its own approval and procedure requirements.

Specify the checks after material removal

Identify the required remaining-thickness, surface-profile and other examination requirements for the actual case. Keep measurement positions linked to the original observation and repair area. Where the final face has a functional or visible role, include the relevant acceptance detail. A thickness measurement outside the repaired zone does not establish the remaining thickness at its deepest location.

Close the record with the supplied condition

Retain the authorised instruction, processing record, final results and accepted disposition. If the repair changes a controlled dimension, transfer the approved change into the appropriate part documentation. Tell the receiving reviewer which area was repaired and what evidence establishes acceptance. This allows the delivery to be reviewed from the completed record rather than from a polished photograph.
