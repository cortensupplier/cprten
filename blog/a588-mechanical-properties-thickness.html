A588 Steel Plate Mechanical Properties: Check the Thickness Basis

How to review A588 yield, tensile and elongation records against the ordered grade and thickness.

How to review A588 yield, tensile and elongation records against the ordered grade and thickness.

Start from the ordered plate

Record the A588 grade, standard edition, product form and ordered thickness before comparing test values. A headline strength number does not describe every thickness and grade condition. The project engineer should set the acceptance basis in the purchase specification.

Review the complete test line

Ask the certificate reviewer to identify the reported yield measure, tensile result, elongation basis and heat or test unit. Compare these against the governing standard and any project supplements. Do not infer compliance from one favorable number while ignoring the other required values.

Keep processing separate

Cutting, welding or forming a plate does not turn the mill test report into a finished-assembly test. If the fabrication scope calls for additional inspection or qualification, list it separately. The material certificate covers the identified supplied material within its stated scope.

Handle discrepancies before release

If the certificate thickness, grade or test line does not match the order, hold the affected pieces and request a documented resolution. Log the decision against the heat and package number so a later cut part is not accepted on a mismatched record.
