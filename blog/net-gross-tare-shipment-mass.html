Net, Gross and Tare Mass on Steel Shipping Records

Keep product mass, packaging mass and weighing evidence distinct across the shipment documents.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Steel coils with banding and identification marks. Photograph for material or application context; component details follow the project drawing.

Steel shipment records often show several mass figures that describe different things. Net mass refers to the contents under the agreed document basis; gross includes the associated packaging, and tare identifies the packaging or container mass being accounted for. Define the basis of each reported figure before reconciling a discrepancy.

Name the level being weighed

State whether a result belongs to a piece, bundle, crate, vehicle or loaded container. Include the package identities covered by the result. A vehicle weighbridge ticket and the sum of individual plate estimates do not measure the same scope. Record any separately loaded timber, accessories or loose components that could otherwise appear as an unexplained difference.

Distinguish calculation from measurement

Mark a geometry-based mass estimate as theoretical and identify the dimensions and density assumption used. Mark a scale result as measured and keep its ticket or report available. Do not silently substitute one for the other because it fits a preferred total. The commercial order should state the agreed quantity and invoicing basis separately from the logistics records.

Reconcile packaging changes

If crates, dunnage or protective supports are added after a preliminary estimate, update the relevant packed-mass information. For a partial shipment, confirm which packages the total includes. Avoid subtracting a generic tare value from every bundle when the packaging arrangements are different. Keep corrections traceable to the document issue they replace.

Example: plates plus a separate accessory crate

The plate list may report only the main pieces while the dispatch total also includes a crate of accessories and support timber. Resolve the apparent difference by matching the scope, not by changing a plate thickness in the calculation. Share the reconciled package totals with the transport team before the load is finalised.

Mass reconciliation fields

ItemInformation to define
ScopePieces or packages included
BasisTheoretical or measured
PackagingTare items included in the figure
EvidenceTicket, calculation and revision

Before you release the next stage

  • Compare figures that cover the same contents.
  • Label theoretical estimates clearly.
  • Update the total after packing changes.
