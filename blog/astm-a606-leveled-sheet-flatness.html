ASTM A606 Leveled Sheet: Flatness at the Delivery Point

Clarify leveling, cut length and flatness acceptance for A606 sheet bundles.

SHANDONG DAZHI7 November 2026

Name the measured product

The buyer may expect a flat blank while the supplier quotes material directly from coil. Specify whether the order is for leveled cut sheets and which dimensions are inspected after conversion. The actual A606 type, thickness and delivered condition belong on the same line.

Set a practical flatness method

State the support surface, sheet orientation, measurement location and acceptance basis agreed by engineering and the processor. A sheet can behave differently when freely laid, clamped or later formed. Avoid rejecting material by a photograph without a repeatable measurement method.

Check cutting effects

Cutting to length can reveal residual shape or edge movement. Ask whether flatness is verified before or after cutting and whether the first and last sheets in a coil require special attention. Document any correction step and its effect on surface appearance.

Preserve the visible face

Leveling equipment can mark a surface that will remain exposed. Identify the face that matters and review a representative sample when appearance is part of acceptance. Separate cosmetic criteria from flatness and dimensional checks so each exception has a clear remedy.

Receive by bundle

A label should show type, heat or coil identity, nominal size, count and package number. Compare a sample of sheets with the approved schedule at receipt, then record any hold or release decision against the bundle.

Send the right information

See the related product inquiry and product directory, or send the approved specification and drawing for a scope review.
