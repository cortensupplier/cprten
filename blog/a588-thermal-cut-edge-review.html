Thermal-Cut ASTM A588 Plate Edges: Purchase and Inspection Scope

Define cut geometry, edge condition and follow-on machining for thermally cut A588 parts.

SHANDONG DAZHI5 November 2026

Set the finished geometry

Give the cutting shop a controlled drawing with thickness, nominal contour, corner radii, cutouts and required edge tolerance. State whether the piece is a near-net blank or a finished component. The process choice may depend on thickness and detail size.

Define downstream work

If an edge will be welded or machined, identify the additional preparation and allowance. A thermally cut profile is not automatically ready for a specific joint or fit. Ask the responsible engineer and fabricator to approve any required treatment or inspection.

Keep part identity through nesting

Transfer heat and part marks before the parent plate is separated. A cut map should reconcile accepted pieces, scrap and remnants. If marks are removed during cleaning, use a controlled alternative identification method.

Inspect before packing

Record dimensions, edge damage and agreed cut condition on representative or specified pieces. Protect critical edges in transit and label each package by drawing revision. This lets the receiving shop distinguish cutting defects from shipping damage.

Cut edge example

A flame-cut blank may meet its rough outside dimensions yet still require machining at a bearing face. Put the rough and finished dimensions on separate drawing layers or schedule columns. Then the cutter, machine shop and inspector can each see which edge condition they own.
