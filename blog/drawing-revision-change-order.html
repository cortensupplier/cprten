Controlling Drawing Changes After a Steel Order

Track the affected parts, work already completed and release status when a fabrication drawing changes.

SHANDONG DAZHI28 October 2026

Identify the affected work

A drawing change needs an impact list, not just an updated PDF. Identify each part mark, quantity and purchase line touched by the change. Ask the fabricator which pieces are uncut, cut, formed, assembled or packed. The same revision may be easy to adopt for untouched stock and require a separate disposition for completed components.

Issue a clear stop and restart instruction

State which work is paused and which unaffected lines may continue. Send the new revision with a readable change description and obtain acknowledgement from the production contact. A file uploaded to a shared folder does not prove that the shop has stopped using a downloaded copy. Agree who authorises the restart after the impact review.

Record the commercial and inspection consequences

Changes can affect material use, processing time, inspection and packing. Ask for those consequences to be stated before accepting the revised scope. Where existing pieces are retained or modified, record their identity and approved disposition. Update the inspection drawing and packing register so the receiving team does not reject an agreed change or accept an obsolete part.

A practical record structure

RecordInformation to agree
Affected partMark and quantity
Work statusUncut, formed or completed
InstructionHold, continue or rework
ReleaseAccepted revision and restart date

Before closing the review

  • List affected parts rather than only drawing numbers.
  • Confirm receipt of the stop instruction.
  • Use the accepted revision for final inspection.
