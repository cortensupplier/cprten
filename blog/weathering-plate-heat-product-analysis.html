Heat Analysis vs Product Analysis on Steel Plate Records

Distinguish producer heat chemistry from analysis of a sampled product and review each against its applicable acceptance requirements.

SHANDONG DAZHI23 November 2026

Heat analysis and product analysis describe different sampling bases. Heat analysis represents the producer analysis associated with a heat; product analysis examines material sampled from a product. Identify the basis before comparing figures or deciding that a difference proves incorrect material.

Ask what material the result represents

Record who issued the analysis, which heat or plate it identifies, where the sample came from and which method was used. A producer certificate and a separate laboratory report may be examining related material under different sampling arrangements. Keep both identities visible. A sample described only as steel plate is difficult to connect to a disputed delivered piece.

Use the acceptance rule for that basis

Check the ordered product standard and relevant general requirements for the treatment of each analysis. Product-analysis provisions or permissible variations must come from those governing documents, not a general internet allowance. Do not add an arbitrary percentage to every chemical limit. Equally, do not assume that a catalogue chemistry table is a complete description of every applicable analysis provision.

Review differences before declaring a cause

Confirm units, decimal notation, reported elements, test method and any stated uncertainty or limitation. Ask the competent laboratory and producer to review a discrepancy against the actual requirements. Surface preparation, sampling identity and the represented material need their own evidence. The website cannot determine the cause of a disagreement from two isolated numbers.

Keep the original records and a clear disposition

Preserve the unaltered reports with the purchase line and sample history. Record which result was assessed under which requirement, any further examination requested and the accepted decision. If a replacement certificate is issued, retain the superseded version with its status identified. This creates an auditable explanation rather than a combined table that silently replaces one party result with another.

Analysis-basis review

Review itemBasis or example
Heat analysisProducer report and identified heat
Product analysisSampled product and sampling record
RequirementsApplicable limits and analysis provisions
ResolutionClarification, further evidence and accepted disposition

A chemical number becomes useful for acceptance only when its material identity and analysis basis are clear.
