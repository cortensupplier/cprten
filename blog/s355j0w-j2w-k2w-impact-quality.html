S355J0W, S355J2W or S355K2W: What Must the Order Preserve?

Keep impact-quality designations, thickness and inspection evidence explicit when comparing S355 weathering plate offers.

SHANDONG DAZHI21 November 2026

The J0, J2 and K2 parts of these grade names identify different impact-quality requirements. They should remain visible in the inquiry, quotation and material records. Selecting between them is a project specification decision; it cannot be resolved by asking only for S355 weathering plate.

Read the suffix as part of the material designation

For the conventional full-size longitudinal Charpy reference, J0 denotes 27 J at 0 degrees Celsius, J2 denotes 27 J at minus 20 degrees Celsius, and K2 denotes 40 J at minus 20 degrees Celsius. Apply the relevant standard edition and its provisions for the actual product, thickness and specimen. These reference values are not a complete material acceptance table or a service-temperature design rule.

Keep strength and impact checks separate

The shared S355 designation does not mean that every plate thickness has one identical yield requirement. Review the thickness range against the ordered standard separately from the impact quality. A tensile-test result cannot replace the required impact evidence, and a successful impact result does not establish the full chemistry, dimensional or delivery-condition requirements.

Check whether the report represents the ordered test

Identify the heat, product, test temperature, specimen information, orientation and reported results. Where the project requires additional testing, include that scope in the order before the inspection arrangement is made. Ask the responsible quality reviewer to resolve a missing field or a different test basis rather than comparing only the most prominent energy number on the page.

Treat a higher-looking suffix as a proposal

If an offer changes the specified quality, obtain an explicit review of the complete material and documentation. Do not approve it automatically because one reference energy appears higher. Confirm the available product form and delivered condition as well. A controlled change should reach the purchase line, drawing material list and inspection package so the receiving team uses one accepted requirement.

Review itemBasis or example
Full designationS355 plus the required impact and W suffixes
Product basisStandard edition, form, thickness and condition
Test evidenceTemperature, specimen basis, orientation and results
