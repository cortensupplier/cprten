ASTM A588 Plate: Split Shop and Site Fabrication Scope

A responsibility matrix for A588 plate supplied as raw material, processed parts or installed assemblies.

SHANDONG DAZHI7 November 2026

Define the handover item

A quotation for A588 plate can mean mill plate, cut blanks, drilled pieces or welded assemblies. State the exact delivered stage on each line. The structural engineer approves the design; the purchase order names which party executes each processing step.

Assign drawings and revisions

The shop needs a released drawing for cutting and drilling. The site team needs installation drawings and as-built changes. Agree who issues revisions, who checks fit and how a changed hole pattern is approved after material has been processed.

Separate qualification records

Mill certificates, welding procedure documents, dimensional inspection and site acceptance serve different purposes. Ask only the responsible party for each record and specify when it is due. A certificate for the plate does not cover an uninspected completed assembly.

Plan visible-face treatment

If the assembly remains unpainted, coordinate shop cleaning, weld appearance, handling marks and any protected zones. If coated, define which coats are applied before dispatch and which are completed after installation.

Close out by part mark

At handover, match every piece or assembly mark to its heat record, drawing revision, inspection outcome and package. The installer should be able to identify missing hardware or a wrong-handed component before lifting it into place.
