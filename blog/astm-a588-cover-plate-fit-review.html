ASTM A588 Cover Plates: Existing-Member Fit and Survey

A buyer checklist for A588 cover plates that must fit an existing steel member rather than a nominal drawing.

SHANDONG DAZHI9 November 2026

Verify the original condition

Collect the original drawing, member location, site photographs and any available material records. A weathered surface does not prove the existing steel is A588. The structural engineer must approve the strengthening detail and the replacement or added material specification.

Survey controlling dimensions

Measure the actual flange or web width, existing holes, welds, obstructions and local curvature. Mark the installation orientation. A cover plate cut to the nominal member size may not fit an older structure with repairs or distortion.

Separate shop and field work

State whether the supplier delivers raw plate, cut blanks, drilled parts or pieces with weld preparation. Identify who removes coatings, checks site fit, installs bolts or performs field welding. The purchase scope should name each handover point.

Resolve contact and drainage

A plate placed against an existing member can create a sheltered crevice. The designer should decide how that interface is protected, drained and inspected. A588's atmospheric designation does not decide this detail by itself.

Close the traceability chain

Mark each plate by location and drawing revision, keep its heat record linked, and record approved fit changes. A first location trial may reveal a systematic error before the full set is fabricated.
