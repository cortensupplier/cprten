Weathering Plate Thickness Mapping: Make Readings Traceable

Record plate thickness readings by location, surface condition and measurement method so a reviewer can interpret the results.

SHANDONG DAZHI23 November 2026

A thickness map connects each reading to an identified location and an agreed measurement method. It supports a clearer investigation than an unlabeled list of values. The map itself does not establish the required tolerance, sampling sufficiency or whether a surface condition has been correctly accounted for.

Draw a location system the receiver can reproduce

Use a plate identifier, a named reference corner and two clearly directed axes. Mark the planned locations on a sketch and identify any extra readings taken around a particular observation. Keep edge locations distinguishable from interior locations. If the plate is turned over during the inspection, preserve the same reference system rather than starting a second unlabeled grid.

Describe the actual measurement method

Record the instrument identity, relevant equipment checks, measurement procedure and surface condition. Mechanical and ultrasonic methods can have different access and preparation considerations. Let the competent inspection party select and confirm the suitable method for the task. Include any limitation that affects interpretation instead of reporting every displayed number as though the setup were identical.

Preserve local readings and their context

Keep the individual observations with their positions. Do not replace a low local value with a plate-wide average without reviewing the governing acceptance provisions. Likewise, one isolated reading does not establish the average thickness of the full plate. Identify whether a reading was taken before or after any agreed surface preparation, grinding or other material-removing operation.

Separate observations from the acceptance decision

Give the reviewer the ordered specification, applicable tolerance basis and measurement map together. If additional locations or repeat readings are needed, retain the earlier results and explain the follow-up. A controlled sequence helps distinguish a setup issue from a material question. Keep the final disposition connected to the specific plate and drawing requirements, not just to the shipment total.

Review itemBasis or example
Plate referenceIdentity, side and reference corner
LocationCoordinates or clearly marked grid point

Keep each thickness reading attached to its location, method and applicable acceptance basis.
