Cold Forming ASTM A588 Plate: What the Drawing Must Resolve

A buyer review of A588 plate grade, thickness, bend geometry and first-piece acceptance.

SHANDONG DAZHI5 November 2026

Identify the material and geometry

Send the exact A588 grade, thickness and standard edition with a finished-part drawing. Mark bend direction, radius, angle, flange length and any later welds. The engineer must approve the proposed forming route and any design change.

Review feasible processing

Ask the fabricator to assess the actual plate condition and available equipment before promising a bend. The permissible geometry cannot be inferred solely from a grade name. A trial piece from representative material can establish tool marks and dimensional outcome.

Protect traceability

A plate may be cut into several blanks before forming. Transfer the heat identity to each piece and record rejects or rework. If the visible face matters, mark its orientation before forming and review any surface change on the first article.

Agree final checks

Inspect the finished radius, angle, straight lengths and any distortion using the approved datums. Link the measurements to the drawing revision and piece mark. A quote should distinguish formed parts from raw plate supply.

Forming approval example

A drawing may show a tight return on a thick plate that cannot be made by the quoted shop route. Resolve the radius and any alternate joint before accepting the price. If a trial blank is used, record its heat, thickness and setup so the approved result can be repeated.
