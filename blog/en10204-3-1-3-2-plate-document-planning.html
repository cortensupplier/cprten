EN 10204 Type 3.1 or 3.2: Plan the Plate Document Route Early

Distinguish inspection-document validation from an ordinary certificate copy or a separate third-party visit.

SHANDONG DAZHI21 November 2026

EN 10204 describes inspection documents; the selected type belongs in the purchase requirements before the order is released. Type 3.1 and Type 3.2 have different validation arrangements. Adding a third-party stamp to an existing report is not, by itself, a complete description of an accepted Type 3.2 route.

Specify the document type and the underlying material

Name the required inspection document alongside the product standard, complete grade, dimensions, delivery condition and any additional tests. The document type does not replace those technical requirements. Identify the intended recipient and approval route, especially if an end customer has its own acceptance conditions. Resolve the proposed producer and inspection arrangements before relying on the document description in a price comparison.

Understand what changes between the types

A Type 3.1 certificate reports specific inspection results and is validated by the manufacturer authorised inspection representative who is independent of the manufacturing department. Type 3.2 adds validation through the purchaser authorised inspection representative or the inspector designated by official regulations, alongside the manufacturer representative. Check the actual applicable standard and agreed route for the order rather than treating these descriptions as permission to issue or relabel documents.

Agree attendance, evidence and timing

Clarify which tests, identity checks or document reviews the agreed route involves and who must witness or validate them. A separate inspection visit can be useful without satisfying every requirement of the requested certificate arrangement. If production has already happened, obtain the purchaser acceptance of any proposed alternative or retrospective approach before describing it as equivalent to the original requirement.

Preserve the supplied-material connection

Keep the heat references, product identification, test records and any cut-piece transfer register connected to the validated documents. An otherwise complete certificate for another heat cannot establish the identity of the delivered plate. When multiple sources or split deliveries are proposed, review the corresponding document package for each material line and resolve any open scope before final acceptance.
