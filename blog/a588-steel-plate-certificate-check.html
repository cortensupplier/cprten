Checking an A588 Steel Plate Certificate Against the Order

Use the purchase line, heat number and test record to review an A588 plate certificate without inferring compliance from a cover page.

SHANDONG DAZHI2 November 2026

Use the purchase line, heat number and test record to review an A588 plate certificate without inferring compliance from a cover page.

Read the identity fields first

Compare the certificate’s material specification, grade, heat number, product form and listed dimensions with the order. Check that the document covers the actual shipped plates or identified cut parts. The word “weathering” in a product description is not a substitute for the specified ASTM designation.

Review the recorded results

Ask a qualified reviewer to compare the reported chemical and mechanical test values with the contracted edition and grade. Do not copy a generic web table into an acceptance decision: grade, thickness and applicable notes can change the required comparison. Keep the certificate and purchase specification together.

Resolve gaps explicitly

If a field is absent, unclear or inconsistent with the packing list, request a corrected or supporting record from the supplier. Record the resolution before releasing the material to fabrication. A certificate from another heat cannot validate an untraceable plate, even if the nominal thickness is the same.

File the decision by lot

Log who reviewed the certificate, which purchase line and package it covers, and any open exception. This creates a practical audit trail when a later part is cut from the same heat or a replacement plate arrives.
