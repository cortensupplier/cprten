Press Brake Marks on Visible Weathering Steel Faces

Use a representative formed sample to agree the appearance of exposed bends and tooling contact areas.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Steel sheet bundles with visible edges and strapping. Photograph for material or application context; component details follow the project drawing.

A formed component can meet its dimensions while showing contact marks that the buyer did not expect. For visible weathering steel, agree the acceptable supplied appearance of tooling contact areas before producing a repeated batch. A flat colour sample cannot demonstrate the appearance of a formed edge.

Identify which faces will remain visible

Mark exposed faces, bend lines and any areas concealed by trims on the drawing. State the condition in which appearance will be assessed: immediately after forming, after the agreed cleaning process or after a specified finish treatment. Avoid judging an untreated production piece against a sample that received a different preparation route.

Let the fabricator review the proposed method

Explain the appearance requirement and ask which forming and protection options are feasible for the actual material, thickness and length. Do not prescribe a protective layer without confirming its suitability for the tooling and process. Any proposed extra finishing should be included in the quotation scope so it is not assumed to be part of a basic bend price.

Measure the released part, not the loaded machine angle

The finished geometry must be checked in the agreed released condition. Steel can recover elastically after bending, so an in-process tool position is not the final angle. Keep the first-piece dimensional check beside the appearance review. An acceptable surface does not waive a dimensional discrepancy, and a correct angle does not settle a disputed visible mark.

Retain a meaningful production reference

Identify the accepted sample by drawing revision, material and process stage. Photograph the relevant contact areas under an agreed viewing arrangement and record the decision. If production tooling or the finishing route changes, review whether the reference still represents the supplied result. Do not promise that subsequent weathering will make every tooling mark disappear.

  • Use a formed sample for formed appearance.
