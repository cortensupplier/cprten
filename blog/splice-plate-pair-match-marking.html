Splice Plate Sets: Pair Identity and Hole Alignment

Keep a splice plate connected to its mating pieces, viewing direction and approved hole pattern.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Steel plate stock with varied surface weathering. Photograph for material or application context; component details follow the project drawing.

A splice plate should be ordered as part of a defined connection set. Matching overall dimensions do not prove that two plates belong together or that their holes align with the intended members. Identify the set, orientation and hole pattern before fabrication begins.

Show the joint as well as the loose plate

Provide the component drawing and an assembly view locating it at the joint. Identify the member ends, plate faces and any top or outside reference. Where plates sit on opposite sides, state whether they are identical, mirrored or individually detailed. The connection designer determines the material, geometry, fasteners and structural requirements; the supply drawing must carry those decisions into production.

Decide whether pieces are interchangeable

Some sets are intended to fit any matching member made to the same drawing. Others are supplied as specifically matched pieces. State which approach applies and whether a trial fit or template check is required. Match marks should be readable at assembly but placed in an approved location. Avoid a marking system that only the packing team understands.

Investigate misalignment before changing holes

When a trial fit fails, first compare the drawing revisions, viewing direction, part marks and measurement datums. A rotated or reversed plate can resemble a machining error. Record the actual discrepancy and refer any proposed hole alteration to the responsible approval route. Enlarging a hole in the workshop is not simply a packing correction.

Pack the connection set coherently

Give each set a contents list and make any separately supplied fasteners or accessories explicit. Keep the set identity on both the packaging and the component register. For a split shipment, flag incomplete sets so the installation team can avoid planning a joint around missing pieces.
