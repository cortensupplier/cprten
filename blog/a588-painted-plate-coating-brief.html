Painted ASTM A588 Plate: Prepare the Coating Brief

Define surface preparation, coating scope and inspection for an A588 project that will be painted.

SHANDONG DAZHI5 November 2026

Name the reason for coating

A588 can be specified for a project that still requires painting. The designer should state which zones are coated, including sheltered or salt-affected details, and which remain bare. Do not assume a weathering designation removes the need for a project exposure review.

Specify the supplied surface

The purchase package should distinguish mill plate, blast-cleaned plate, shop-primed parts and fully coated assemblies. State who owns surface preparation and when welding or drilling occurs. A primer applied before further fabrication may need a defined repair plan.

Coordinate the coating system

The coatings specialist should set preparation grade, profile, compatible products, thickness and inspection method. The material supplier should quote only the scope it can document. Record masking at faying surfaces or weld zones where the approved detail requires it.

Check the handover

Ask for part identity, preparation and coating records tied to the drawing and package marks. Confirm how transit damage and site touch-up are handled. Do not accept a generic “painted A588” description as a complete finish specification.

Coating scope example

A fabricator may quote blast cleaning and primer while the site contractor expects a complete multi-coat finish. List the preparation, shop coats, field coats and repair responsibility by party. Include the state at handover, because a coated plate can still require work after welding or installation.
