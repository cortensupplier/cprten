Weathering Steel Sculpture Bases, Anchors and Concrete Interfaces

Coordinate sculpture base plates, embedded plates, anchors, access and drainage with the foundation designer before fabrication.

SHANDONG DAZHI / BUYER RESOURCESUpdated 27 October 20264 min read

Connect the sculpture geometry to the foundation design

A sculpture base needs an approved load path from the steelwork into its support. The sculpture fabricator, structural designer and foundation contractor should agree their responsibilities before plate is cut. The steel grade and a product photograph do not establish base stability or anchorage capacity.

Provide the sculpture geometry, support arrangement and installation location to the responsible designer. Identify whether the supplied steelwork includes only a base plate or also brackets, anchor components, templates and embedded plates. List any installation items supplied by another contractor.

Set one datum for anchors and base plates

Show the anchor pattern, plate outline, orientation and reference datums on controlled drawings. Mark dimensions to the actual hole centres and identify the viewing direction. A rotated or mirrored pattern can fit the plate drawing while conflicting with the installed foundation.

Coordinate tolerances between the steel plate, anchor template and concrete work. Decide how foundation measurements are accepted before dispatch. A trial template can be useful for fit review, but it does not replace engineering acceptance of the anchor design or concrete installation.

Detail the concealed concrete interface

For embedded plates, show the concrete face, steel projection, weld access and required installation sequence. Identify the surfaces that become inaccessible after casting or assembly and record any specified preparation before closure. Changes to an embedded plate require review before the concrete operation.

Trace water at the top of the base, around the plate edges and beneath covers. Avoid concealed pockets that collect debris or remain wet. Any grout, coating, isolation or drainage detail belongs in the approved interface design and should be coordinated with the selected materials.

Plan access and acceptance before installation

Check that the installation team can reach the fasteners and inspection points after the sculpture is placed. Agree any temporary supports and handling arrangements with the responsible contractor. Do not modify anchor holes or add welded attachments as an unreviewed solution to a site mismatch.

The handover pack should identify the base plate, anchors, approved drawings, material records and accepted deviations. Include access instructions for inspecting drainage and concealed interfaces so future maintenance can find the critical locations.

Before releasing the order

  • Assign responsibility for the foundation and steel interface.
  • Use shared datums and verify anchor orientation.
  • Approve concealed surfaces and drainage details.
  • Resolve fit discrepancies before installation and retain records.
