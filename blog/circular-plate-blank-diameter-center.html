Circular Steel Blanks: Diameter, Centre Holes and Inspection Datums

Specify a circular weathering steel blank by its finished outline and the relationship between its features.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Steel plates showing flat faces and cut edges. Photograph for material or application context; component details follow the project drawing.

A round plate is not fully defined by a diameter and thickness. The order also needs to distinguish a finished disc from a rough blank, and to explain how any centre hole relates to the outside edge. These choices determine what the processor cuts and what the buyer measures.

Start with the delivered condition

Show whether the circular edge will be used as supplied or machined later. For a rough blank, put the machining allowance on the blank drawing and keep the finished part drawing available for context. For a finished disc, state the permitted dimensional variation and edge condition explicitly. Do not use the same nominal diameter for both stages without explaining the difference.

Locate the centre feature deliberately

A centre hole may be a lifting-related feature, an assembly reference or a machining setup feature; its purpose changes the questions that need answering. Give its size and positional relationship to the agreed datum. A disc can have an acceptable outside diameter while its hole is misplaced. If concentricity or runout matters, the responsible designer must define the required control and measurement basis.

Agree how the edge is checked

Tell the inspector which edge condition is final and whether measurements precede or follow deburring. Compare diameter measurements in more than one direction when checking the specified outline. Keep local edge damage, dimensional variation and a centre-position discrepancy as separate observations. A single tape measurement across an arbitrary chord is not an adequate description of the part.

Example: a blank for later turning

If the customer will turn the outside edge and bore the hole, identify both as unfinished features. Ask the processor to confirm the available stock around the intended finished outline. The receiving check can then assess the supplied blank against its own drawing rather than reject it for not already matching the machined component.

Features to place on the disc drawing

ItemInformation to define
Outside edgeBlank or finished diameter and tolerance
Centre holeSize, position and processing stage
Plate conditionGrade, thickness and required records
Inspection basisDatums and final edge condition

Before you release the next stage

  • Label unfinished features on the drawing.
  • Keep hole position separate from outside diameter.
  • State the accepted inspection setup.
