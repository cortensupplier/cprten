MATERIAL BUYER GUIDE

Forming ASTM A606 Sheet: Drawing and Sample Review

Define bend geometry, surface direction and acceptance before forming A606 sheet components.

SHANDONG DAZHI5 November 2026

Give the fabricator a developed drawing

State the A606 type, thickness, rolling condition and finished bend geometry. Include inside radius, flange lengths, bend direction and datums. The designer must approve any radius or material change; a shop should not infer it from an appearance rendering.

Check the process against the actual sheet

Ask the fabricator to review tooling and a representative first piece when geometry is demanding. A cut coupon or sample should come from a relevant material and condition. Record cracking, marking and dimensional fit before releasing repeated parts.

Control exposed faces

Mark the face intended to remain visible and any permitted tool contact. Where formed edges meet adjacent panels, specify gap and drain details. Bending may alter the appearance of a preweathered surface, so agree the supplied finish stage.

Write the inspection method

Measure the agreed radius, angle and flange datums on the finished part. Keep the drawing revision and heat or bundle identity with the inspected item. Rejecting a formed part by a visual impression alone is difficult without an agreed sample or tolerance.

First-piece question

If a long return must align with an adjacent panel, define whether angle, flange length or overall envelope is the controlling fit dimension. Ask the fabricator which dimension will be measured after springback. Put the answer on the approved drawing so repeated parts are made to the same interpretation.
