ASTM A606 vs ASTM A588: Choose the Correct Product Form

A purchasing comparison of ASTM A606 sheet and strip with ASTM A588 structural steel for projects using both thin panels and plate parts.

SHANDONG DAZHI6 November 2026

Begin with the designed component

A project may use thin cladding and heavy connection plates in the same weathering steel assembly. Those parts should not be ordered under one broad “corten steel” line. The designer needs to assign a material specification and product form to each component before the buyer requests prices.

Keep the specification lines separate

ASTM A606 concerns sheet and strip products, while ASTM A588 is used for structural steel products including plate. The order should also state the applicable type or grade, edition, thickness and supplied condition. A stockist's label does not convert an A606 coil into an A588 plate.

Compare processing as well as material

A606 sheet may need leveling, slitting, forming or perforation. A588 plate may need thermal cutting, beveling, drilling or welding. The quote should separate raw material from each processing step so the buyer can compare delivered usable parts rather than nominal tonnes.

Review the joint between them

If an A606 panel attaches to an A588 frame, define the fixing material, hole geometry, movement allowance and drainage. The project engineer should review loads and any exposed or coated zones. A shared weathering appearance does not establish a complete connection design.

Close the handover record

Use a bill of materials with a unique material line per part family. Ask for certificates and piece marks that identify the correct heat, type or grade. Receiving staff can then check an A606 panel bundle and an A588 plate kit without guessing from their surface color.
