MATERIAL BUYER GUIDE

ASTM A606 Expanded Screen RFQ: Aperture, Direction and Border

Specify an A606-based expanded screen by opening geometry, strand direction, perimeter design and a physical trial.

SHANDONG DAZHI10 November 2026

Do not call every open panel “perforated”

Perforated sheet has discrete removed holes. Expanded metal is slit and stretched, leaving connected strands. The processes produce different edge conditions, stiffness and visual rhythm. If the drawing says “mesh” while the quotation assumes drilled holes, the bids are not comparable. Name the process first.

Orient the opening

Show the long-way opening and short-way opening directions on an elevation with a clear top arrow. A rectangular panel turned through ninety degrees can change its appearance and bending response even when its outside dimensions are unchanged. Mark the visible face and confirm whether the mesh is flattened after expansion.

Ask for a representative trial

Expansion is a forming operation, so the starting sheet grade, type, thickness and delivered condition matter. A606 covers sheet and strip; specify the exact approved type and edition. Request a small representative panel in the intended geometry and review broken strands, edge burrs, flatness and handling rigidity. A material certificate for the sheet does not replace finished-panel acceptance.

Draw the border and attachment

Define any unexpanded border, frame, weld, fastener hole and support spacing. An open mesh cannot be quoted solely by outside width and height when the edge has to land on a fixing rail. The designer must approve loading, restraint and weather exposure. Include drainage at the lower edge so debris and water do not collect behind a closed return.

Compare the same deliverable

Request a price for the panel blank and, if needed, a separate price for framing and finish. Then compare sample approval, inspection and packing on the same basis. See the expanded screen inquiry page.
