ASTM A606 Sheet vs Coil: Build a Comparable RFQ

Define product form, type, thickness and processing scope when requesting A606 sheet or coil.

SHANDONG DAZHI5 November 2026

Representative coil photograph for product-form context; it does not identify or certify an ASTM A606 heat.

Separate the supply forms

ASTM A606 is used for sheet and strip. A quote for a master coil does not deliver the same thing as cut sheets or leveled blanks. Put the required type, nominal thickness, width, length or coil mass and quantity on separate purchase lines.

Name the processing sequence

If material arrives in coil, ask who will slit, level and cut it. State edge condition, flatness target, sheet length and how processing waste is charged. If the supplier quotes cut lengths, confirm whether the cut tolerance applies before or after leveling.

Plan handling and identity

Coils and sheets need different packing, lifting and receiving arrangements. Ask how the heat and coil identification is preserved through slitting and cutting. Where the project needs traceability to individual sheets, specify the marking or bundle map before processing.

Compare the same commercial scope

Review gross incoming mass, net delivered mass, processing charges and packaging as separate items. A lower per-tonne coil offer may still require local conversion and handling. Record the agreed type and certificate requirements with the final purchase line.

Quote comparison example

Supplier A offers a master coil while Supplier B offers leveled cut sheets. Put both on a common schedule showing net usable sheet count, waste, leveling responsibility and delivered package size. The purchasing decision can then reflect the work still required at the destination, not simply the initial price per tonne.
