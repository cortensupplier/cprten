A Steel Sample Parcel Does Not Define Production Delivery

Keep sample acceptance, representative scope and later shipment requirements connected without treating them as identical.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Weathered plate surfaces and stacked plate edges. Photograph for material or application context; component details follow the project drawing.

A courier sample can help resolve a material or appearance question, but it is a different delivery from the production order. Identify what the sample represents and carry only the accepted decisions into the later supply scope. Small-parcel convenience should not obscure the requirements of full-size parts.

State the sample purpose

Describe whether the sample is for appearance, dimensional fit, processing review or a specified test. Give it an identifier and record its material and preparation stage where relevant. A small flat coupon cannot demonstrate every feature of a large formed panel. State which questions remain open so the sample decision does not unintentionally become blanket production approval.

Agree what happens to the sample

Identify the recipient, review process and whether the piece will be retained, returned or used in a destructive check. If several parties need to inspect it, plan the sequence before shipment. Keep the sample label readable after unpacking. Photographs should show the identifier as well as the relevant face so later comments can be tied to the correct piece.

Record a decision the workshop can use

Write down the accepted characteristics and any conditions or limitations. Refer to the sample in the production documents where appropriate, while retaining the dimensional and material requirements that the sample does not establish. If the production route differs from the sample preparation, review whether the earlier decision still represents the proposed supply.

Plan production packing separately

Full-size panels or plate bundles need their own package design, handling review and delivery information. Reconfirm the production receiving contact and required documents rather than copying the courier label. Keep the sample reference in the order file so the receiving team knows which approved feature it can compare and which checks need separate evidence.

Before you release the next stage

  • Give every sample a defined purpose.
  • Record the limits of the approval.
  • Prepare the production delivery as its own shipment.
