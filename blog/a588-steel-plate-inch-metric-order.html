A588 BUYER GUIDE

A588 Steel Plate Orders with Inch and Metric Drawings

Prevent unit conversion errors in A588 plate thickness, cut dimensions and test documentation.

SHANDONG DAZHI3 November 2026

Prevent unit conversion errors in A588 plate thickness, cut dimensions and test documentation.

Choose a governing unit system

ASTM A588/A588M uses inch and metric designation. Ask the project team which purchase and drawing units control the order. Do not silently convert a nominal imperial thickness to the closest metric stock label; record the actual ordered nominal value and approved tolerance basis.

Mark every dimension source

A fabrication packet may mix architectural metric elevations with an imperial structural detail. Put units beside each plate thickness, blank size, hole centre and bevel. Add a conversion note only where the engineer has approved it. An unlabeled number should stop the RFQ review.

Keep certificate values traceable

The mill certificate may report tests in another unit system. The reviewer should compare results against the contracted standard edition and grade using a documented conversion, without altering the original certificate. The delivered heat and piece identity remain more important than a converted summary.

Reconcile the order before release

Use a single table showing drawing callout, governing nominal size, quoted size and final cut size. Resolve differences with a revised drawing or written clarification before the plate is cut. This prevents a seemingly small rounding decision from becoming a fit-up issue on site.
