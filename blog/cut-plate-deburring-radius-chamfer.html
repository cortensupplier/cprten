Deburring, Edge Radius or Chamfer: Specify the Cut Plate Finish

Distinguish burr removal from a dimensioned radius or chamfer when ordering finished weathering steel plate edges.

SHANDONG DAZHI23 November 2026

Deburring, rounding and chamfering describe different edge-finishing tasks. A requirement to remove burrs does not automatically establish a controlled radius or chamfer. Specify which edges need treatment and how the finished condition will be accepted so offers cover the same scope.

Label the edge treatment on the drawing

Show the affected edges, including internal openings and any accessible reverse face. Distinguish edges that remain as cut from edges receiving further processing. If a feature needs a measured radius or chamfer, give the required geometry and permitted variation. Avoid a general note saying smooth all edges when some edges must retain a different approved preparation.

Keep function and supplied condition connected

An exposed touch edge, a coating-related preparation and a weld preparation can require different finished details. Use the project-approved requirement for each function. Ask the processor to review access and feasibility on the actual thickness and shape. A photograph of one acceptable outside edge cannot demonstrate that a narrow internal opening has received the same treatment.

Identify when the dimensions apply

Edge finishing can remove material from the outline or opening. State whether the inspection dimensions describe the as-cut feature or its final supplied condition. Where a small component has little remaining material near a hole, refer proposed changes to the accepted drawing process. Do not let an informal workshop interpretation alter a controlled mounting or connection feature.

Agree the inspection evidence

Use a defined visual or dimensional check appropriate to the specified treatment. Identify representative and critical edges on the inspection schedule, and record any separately agreed sample. If the buyer needs a particular tactile or appearance outcome, describe the review conditions as well. Do not substitute a claim of deburred for evidence of a dimensioned profile that the order actually requires.

Edge-finishing scope checklist

Review itemBasis or example
LocationExternal edges, openings and relevant faces
TreatmentBurr removal, radius, chamfer or other preparation
GeometrySpecified dimensions where required
AcceptanceFinal stage and agreed inspection method
