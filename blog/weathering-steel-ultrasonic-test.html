Specifying Ultrasonic Examination for Weathering Steel Plate

Define the examination standard, extent, acceptance level, timing and reporting before ordering ultrasonic testing of plate.

SHANDONG DAZHI / BUYER RESOURCESUpdated 27 October 20264 min read

Define the question that the examination must answer

Ultrasonic examination is a separate inspection requirement that should be specified by the purchaser or responsible engineer when the project needs it. A request for weathering steel, a particular grade or a general material certificate does not by itself define an ultrasonic acceptance requirement.

Identify the relevant plate thickness, intended component and critical fabrication features. The design team should decide whether examination is required and which standard and acceptance level apply. Avoid advertising an unspecified scan as proof that a plate is defect free or suitable for every structural use.

State the standard and extent of coverage

Write the examination standard, edition and acceptance level on the purchase order. Define whether coverage is full plate, an agreed scan pattern, edge zones or specifically marked areas. The method and acceptance basis must be compatible with the ordered material and project specification.

The examination procedure should address equipment, calibration, scanning, surface condition and reporting under the selected standard. These are inspection-provider responsibilities subject to the project agreement. A buyer should request the procedure and acceptance reference instead of inventing probe settings from a product webpage.

Agree when the plate will be examined

Examination before cutting may serve a different purpose from examination around a finished part or welded assembly. State the required stage and how the inspected area will remain identifiable after processing. If an indication affects a proposed cut blank, resolve the disposition before the part is released.

Arrange handling and access so the examination can be performed at the agreed stage. Include any required witness or hold point in the schedule. Testing performed after shipment may not meet an order that required purchaser review before dispatch.

Keep the report connected to the supplied material

Ask the report to identify the plate or part, heat number, dimensions, examination date, procedure, operator identification and acceptance result as required by the governing documents. Where indications are recorded, keep their locations understandable against the inspection drawing.

Review the report alongside the material certificate and dimensional records. Each answers a different acceptance question. If a report names another plate, omits the selected acceptance level or has no traceable material identity, request clarification before treating it as evidence for the shipment.

Before releasing the order

  • Obtain the project examination requirement.
  • Specify method, extent, stage and acceptance level.
  • Preserve inspected-area identity after cutting.
  • Review the traceable report before release where required.
