Planning the Handling of Weathering Steel Plate and Panels

Coordinate mass, geometry, finish protection, approved lifting arrangements and transfer records for steel plate and fabricated panels.

SHANDONG DAZHI / BUYER RESOURCESUpdated 27 October 20264 min read

Provide the handling contractor with the actual load

Handling starts with reliable information about the supplied component. Give the responsible contractor the dimensions, mass, geometry, centre-of-gravity information where required, and the delivery condition. A flat plate, folded tray and assembled sculpture need different handling arrangements even if their base material is the same.

Identify the receiving area and the sequence from unloading to storage and installation. Access, support locations and the availability of the planned lifting equipment should be resolved before dispatch. Treat handling as a planned contractor operation rather than assuming any convenient opening can be used as a lifting point.

Use an approved lifting arrangement

The qualified handling team should select and approve equipment and attachment points for the actual load. Product photos and online plate-weight estimates do not establish lifting capacity. Do not add temporary lifting holes, welded lugs or modifications without the required engineering and fabrication approval.

Where designated attachment points are provided, show them on the controlled handling drawing and identify any limitations. Keep temporary components, removal requirements and any subsequent inspection linked to that drawing. Changes to the load configuration require review of the arrangement.

Protect the agreed surface and geometry

Show visible faces, folded edges and areas that must not bear against transport or lifting equipment. Agree suitable protective contact materials and support positions with the handling team. The purpose is to preserve the accepted finish and shape without interfering with the approved lifting method.

Record condition before loading and after unloading. For thin or formed panels, check returns and corners for deformation as well as visible scratches. Separate acceptable surface variation from mechanical damage; do not promise that weathering will correct a bent return or an impact mark.

Hand over the load with its documents

The packing list should identify component marks, bundle contents and any agreed handling instructions. Keep instructions accessible to the receiving contractor, not hidden inside an inaccessible bundle. Confirm that transport supports can be removed in the planned sequence.

At receipt, document identity, packaging condition and any discrepancy before the material moves to installation. Use a hold-and-review process for damage that could affect fit or the approved handling points. The responsible contractor controls the lifting operation and must follow the applicable site requirements.

Before releasing the order

  • Provide actual component mass and geometry.
  • Obtain an approved handling and lifting arrangement.
  • Mark finish-sensitive faces and permitted support locations.
  • Record condition and identity at every handover.
