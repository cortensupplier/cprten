A588 Steel Plate Receiving Inspection: A Practical Sequence

Check shipment identity, dimensions, condition and records before releasing A588 plates to fabrication.

SHANDONG DAZHI2 November 2026

Check shipment identity, dimensions, condition and records before releasing A588 plates to fabrication.

Start with the order and package list

Match purchase order lines to package marks, piece counts and declared dimensions before opening bundles. Photograph any shipping damage or missing identification on arrival. Keep the material separated from similar weathering grades until the identity check is complete.

Inspect the actual plate

Record visible surface condition, edge damage and obvious distortion. Measure the required dimensions using the agreed method and tolerance basis. A brown surface is not a material test, and a clean surface does not disprove that a plate is A588; appearance is a separate acceptance question.

Link records to pieces

Check that heat marks or controlled bundle identifiers lead to the correct certificate. If cutting or fabrication was ordered, compare part marks and drawing revision as well. Note every discrepancy against the purchase line rather than on a loose email thread.

Release or hold deliberately

Assign an owner and disposition for shortages, unmarked pieces or disputed dimensions. Do not consume affected material before the buyer and supplier resolve the exception. A short receiving report provides the starting point for traceable fabrication and later claims.
