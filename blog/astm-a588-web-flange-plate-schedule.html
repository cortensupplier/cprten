ASTM A588 Web and Flange Plate Schedules: Avoid Mixing Blank Requirements

Build a fabrication-ready A588 web and flange plate list with positions, splice allowances, edge preparation and traceability.

SHANDONG DAZHI10 November 2026

One girder is several purchase lines

A drawing may identify the finished girder but leave the raw plate purchase list implicit. The web, top flange, bottom flange and local reinforcement can have different thicknesses, widths and delivery lengths. Put each blank on a separate schedule line with the member mark and final position. This makes quotation, nesting and receipt checks possible without guessing from a general arrangement drawing.

Use the correct dimensional basis

Distinguish a finished component dimension from the ordered blank dimension. If shop trimming, weld shrinkage allowance or subsequent machining is expected, the approved fabricator should define that allowance. A supplier should not add an arbitrary extra length to every item. Record whether the stated width includes edge machining and whether a splice zone needs a specific cut orientation.

Keep grade and test requirements attached

Write the exact A588 grade and standard edition into the purchase line. Add any project-specific test or inspection requirement before pricing. The structural engineer should confirm whether a different bridge material specification is required; similar weathering appearance is not a substitute for that decision.

Prepare a traceability map

Ask how the heat identifier will survive cutting and how each blank will be linked to the final girder. For a multi-heat assembly, a simple table of member mark, plate position, blank ID and heat number is more useful than a loose stack of certificates. Keep this table aligned with the packing list so receiving staff can locate the right blank.

Before release

Freeze the revision of the member drawing, reconcile every scheduled item to the fabrication bill and obtain approval for any substitution. The related blank inquiry page lists the minimum details to send.
