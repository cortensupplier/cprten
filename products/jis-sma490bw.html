A complete JIS material reference

SMA490BW identifies a weathering steel grade within JIS G 3114 for welded structural applications. Retain the full designation when preparing a material schedule or quotation. SMA490BW should not be shortened to a generic “corten plate” description or automatically exchanged for SPA-H.

SHANDONG DAZHI reviews plate inquiries against the requested standard, dimensions, delivery condition, inspection requirements and fabrication drawings. Supply route, origin, sizes and lead time are confirmed for the individual order.

Define the required plate and records

ItemWhat to state

Delivery conditionRequired condition and any processing limitations from the drawing
InspectionMechanical and impact requirements, heat traceability and supplementary tests where specified
The “490” designation is not a blanket yield-strength guarantee. Required mechanical values depend on the standard, product and thickness range.

Welded structures and unpainted use

The W variant relates to unpainted-use material requirements. It does not approve every outdoor environment. Review drainage, sheltered surfaces, moisture retention and chloride exposure with the project engineer, together with rust-runoff control and any coating strategy.

For welded components, submit connection drawings and the specified inspection level. Fabrication procedures and welding consumables need their own review. Modified producer designations with extra suffixes should be evaluated separately rather than treated as the same order description.

Build a clear SMA490BW RFQ

  • Use the complete JIS G 3114 SMA490BW reference on each applicable order line.
  • Provide dimensions, quantities, delivery condition and drawings.
  • Identify impact tests or other supplementary requirements explicitly.
  • Specify visible surfaces, processing, traceability after cutting and export packing.
  • Include destination, delivery basis and requested schedule.

Read the SMA490BW versus SPA-H guide and the SPA-H product reference before discussing a substitution. A JIS designation does not itself establish Japanese manufacture; confirm origin in the offer and documents.

Is SMA490BW equivalent to SPA-H?

No automatic equivalence is established. They belong to different JIS standards; any substitution needs a technical comparison and project approval.

Does W mean the plate needs no environmental review?

No. Suitability for bare exposure depends on the site, drainage, detailing and project acceptance.

Can cutting or processing be included?

Send the drawing, edge requirements and tolerances. Scope and capability are confirmed in the quotation.
