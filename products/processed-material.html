What to include in an inquiry

  • Base steel grade and applicable product standard
  • Thickness, dimensions, quantity and drawing revision
  • Cutting, bending, hole pattern or assembly requirements
  • Finish expectations, sample approval and acceptable variation
  • Installation context, drainage and runoff constraints
  • Destination, packing and requested delivery schedule
Weathering appearance changes with exposure and drainage. Photos are illustrative; the agreed material specification, approved drawings and finish sample control the order.

Processing methods, dimensional limits, records, availability and lead time are confirmed for each quotation. No standard stock range or fabrication capacity is implied.
