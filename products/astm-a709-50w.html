A bridge-specific material inquiry

ASTM A709/A709M covers structural steel for bridges. Grade 50W is a weathering-steel designation within that specification, with enhanced atmospheric corrosion resistance. Identify both the standard and the exact grade when requesting a quotation.

Bridge procurement can include notch-toughness provisions and supplementary requirements as applicable to the member and project. Provide the bridge owner's specification with the inquiry so acceptance requirements can be reviewed before an order is placed.

Grade 50W and HPS 50W are separate designations. ASTM A588 certification alone should not be presented as proof that an ASTM A709 Grade 50W order has been met.

Prepare a complete ASTM A709 Grade 50W inquiry

Start with the approved plate schedule and bridge specification. If cut blanks are required, include the drawing revision and define how identification must be maintained after processing.

  • Exact Grade 50W designation and governing ASTM edition
  • Dimensions, quantities, unit system and drawing references
  • Member classification and applicable toughness-test requirements
  • Supplementary requirements and project inspection documents
  • Cutting allowances, edge requirements and traceability instructions

Send your destination, delivery basis and requested schedule with the size list. Processing scope, available sizes, inspection documents and lead time are confirmed against the enquiry before an order is agreed.

Bridge fabrication inquiry contexts

  • Plate blanks for bridge girder fabrication
  • Bridge connection plates and structural components where specified
  • Replacement or new-build bridge plate schedules approved by the designer

The bridge designer determines material suitability, fabrication and detailing. Review chloride exposure, persistent dampness, drainage and drying conditions before adopting an uncoated weathering-steel solution.

Frequently asked questions

Is Grade 50W identical to HPS 50W?

No. They are separate designations in the bridge specification. Quote and order the exact grade on the approved material schedule.

Can ASTM A588 automatically replace A709 Grade 50W?

No automatic substitution is implied. Any alternative must satisfy the governing bridge requirements and receive the required project approval.

What should accompany the plate size list?

Include the bridge-owner specification, member classification, toughness requirements, supplementary tests and required traceability documents so the quotation can address the full order.
