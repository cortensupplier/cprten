ASTM A588 Plate-Cut Profiles

Request A588 irregular plate profiles with controlled outlines, internal cutouts, orientation, edge finishing and parent-material traceability.

SHANDONG DAZHIUpdated 27 November 2026

Parent plate stock before component processing. The profile, holes and finished dimensions follow the approved drawing.

These A588 profiles are components cut from an agreed parent plate to a controlled two-dimensional outline. They address inquiries for irregular plate shapes, including contours and internal openings. This supply description concerns plate-cut parts; rolled beams, channels or angles require their own section and product specification.

Start from the complete profile drawing

Provide a readable dimensioned drawing and the agreed production geometry where available. Identify the drawing revision, material grade, thickness, part mark and quantity. Include internal cutouts and their corner geometry rather than leaving them to an automatic toolpath. A sketch of the outside silhouette cannot establish critical hole positions, edge distances or the fit of a mating component.

Control the contour and processing stage

State whether the delivered outline is finished or includes allowance for later machining. Give the accepted dimensional and edge-finish requirements, including any deburring, radius or chamfer. Ask the processor to confirm the proposed cutting route for the actual shape and thickness. Keep cut edges distinct from any subsequent weld preparation or machined reference face.

Preserve orientation and part identity

Identify any required relationship between the parent rolling direction and the component geometry before nesting. Mark handed or mirrored profiles separately. Connect each required cut part to its parent material record through the agreed register or labels. For repeated outlines with different holes or thicknesses, use separate marks so similar appearance does not lead to mixed assemblies.

Compare the completed profile supply

The quotation should identify material allocation, contour cutting, secondary operations, inspection and packing. Agree the treatment of remnants or separately supplied offcuts where relevant. State whether templates or trial fits are required and which components they represent. Structural suitability and connection capacity follow the accepted project design; the manufacturing inquiry should reproduce that controlled geometry and material requirement.

Product questions

Does this page offer hot-rolled A588 steel shapes?

It describes profiles cut from plate. A request for a rolled section needs a separate section designation, dimensions and confirmed product proposal.

What files should accompany the inquiry?

Send the approved dimensioned drawing, the corresponding production geometry if available, and a part schedule with material, thickness and quantities.
