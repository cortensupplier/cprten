A588 PLATE PRODUCT INQUIRY

ASTM A588 Drilled Connection Plates

A588 connection plates with drawing-controlled hole patterns, orientation and assembly identity.

Material and purchase basis

Connection plates need the approved structural drawing, not a hole count alone. Provide plate size, grade, thickness, hole centres, edge distances and any slot direction. The structural designer controls connection capacity.

Define the processing

Separate plate cutting, drilling, deburring and hardware supply in the quotation. State whether the plates are paired, match-drilled or supplied as individual pieces. A weathering steel plate designation does not specify the bolts.

Identification and handover

Package matched sets with a common assembly mark. Record hole inspections against the drawing revision and preserve heat traceability through drilling and dispatch.

Send these details

  • Approved connection drawing
  • Hole pattern and tolerance
  • Hardware inclusion or exclusion
  • Assembly marks and inspection
Availability, processing capacity, size, price and lead time are confirmed after review of the approved specification and drawing. This page does not claim live stock or a certified performance rating.
