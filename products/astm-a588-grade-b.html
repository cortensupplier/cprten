ASTM A588 weathering plate overview

ASTM A588/A588M covers high-strength, low-alloy structural steel with improved atmospheric corrosion resistance for welded, riveted or bolted construction. SHANDONG DAZHI accepts Grade A plate inquiries and reviews the requested specification, dimensions and supply scope before quotation.

The standard covers shapes, plates and bars and limits its scope to material up to 8 inches [200 mm] thick. This is a standard scope limit, not a statement of available stock. ASTM describes the specification as providing up to a 50 ksi [345 MPa] minimum yield point; use the applicable thickness and grade requirements instead of applying one strength value to every plate.

Applications and exposure

  • Bridges and infrastructure, when accepted under the project's bridge material specification.
  • Outdoor structural steelwork and fabricated plate components.
  • Building components and engineered assemblies calling for ASTM A588.

Unpainted use requires suitable atmospheric exposure and detailing. Review drainage, water traps, salt exposure and staining of adjacent materials. A588 is not a blanket bridge approval: a project may require a bridge-specific standard, notch toughness or other acceptance provisions.

Compare Corten B and S355J2W only against the complete project requirements. ASTM A242 is a separate specification. Similar colour or a shared weathering-steel description does not establish substitution.

Frequently asked questions

Is A588 the same as Corten steel?

Corten is often used as a general market term, while ASTM A588 is a material specification with defined requirements. Verify the exact grade and certificate; do not rely on the name or surface appearance.

Does every A588 plate have a 345 MPa minimum yield strength?

Do not apply that value universally. Strength requirements depend on the applicable thickness and specification provisions. Confirm the required values for the plate being ordered.

Is Grade A included in the quotation?

State Grade A explicitly if it is required. The offer and mill documentation should identify the agreed grade, standard edition, size and delivery condition.

How is ASTM A588 plate priced?

Price depends on specification, dimensions, quantity, processing, inspection, finish and delivery. Send a size list and destination so that the quotation can define each inclusion.
