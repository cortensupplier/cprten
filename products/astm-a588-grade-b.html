Ordering ASTM A588 Grade B plate

ASTM A588/A588M includes distinct grade designations. An enquiry for Grade B should identify that grade explicitly on the quotation, purchase order and inspection records. A general description such as weathering steel or A588 alone does not establish the requested grade.

This page is for buyers whose drawings or purchase specification call for Grade B. For a general introduction to the standard, see the ASTM A588 plate guide. Confirm proposed substitutions with the project designer before ordering.

Project uses and acceptance

Enquiries may cover outdoor steelwork, fabricated components or infrastructure plate specified as A588 Grade B. Project acceptance depends on the drawings, design requirements and material documentation. A bridge project may have separate toughness or bridge-standard requirements; the grade name alone does not establish compliance.

For exposed weathering steel, review drainage, persistent moisture and salt exposure with the project team. See our exposure and coastal-site checklist and weathering steel plate products.

Prepare a Grade B quotation request

  1. Attach the material schedule and specify Grade B on every applicable line.
  2. Separate full plates, cut blanks and fabricated parts, with quantities and drawings.
  3. List certificate, traceability and testing requirements before comparing prices.
  4. State destination, packing requirements and the requested delivery terms.

Availability, processing scope and lead time must be confirmed in the quotation. Use the material certificate checklist to review the proposed documentation.

Grade B ordering questions

Can Grade A be supplied instead of Grade B?

Do not assume the two grades are interchangeable. Any alternative needs review against the purchase specification and approval by the responsible project team.

Does a rusted surface prove the plate is Grade B?

No. Appearance cannot verify grade identity. Check the material designation, heat number and inspection documentation.

Can I request cut-to-size Grade B blanks?

Include a cutting list or drawing with the enquiry. The quotation should confirm processing feasibility, tolerances and how material traceability will be maintained.
