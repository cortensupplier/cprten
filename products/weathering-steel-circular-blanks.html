DRAWING-DEFINED PLATE COMPONENTS

Weathering Steel Circular Plate Blanks

Circular discs and ring blanks cut from specified weathering steel plate, with finished or machining-allowance dimensions agreed by drawing.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Steel plate stock shown for material context. See the geometry schematic and approved project drawing for the component outline.

Request circular plate blanks as drawing-defined components for further fabrication or machining. The inquiry should identify the parent material, thickness, outside diameter and any central opening. Plain discs, annular blanks and profiles with additional features are different cutting scopes and should have their own part marks.

Choose a disc or an annular blank

Show the complete outside profile and any internal opening. For a ring, specify both diameters and the required relationship between them. Identify whether slots, bolt holes or other features belong in the supplied blank. A sketch showing only the outside diameter does not establish a centre opening, an edge preparation or a hole pattern.

State what happens after delivery

If the blank will be turned or bored, provide its supplied dimensions and the required machining allowance separately from the final part dimensions. For a finished cut component, state the accepted size variation and edge condition. The proposed processing method, available material and achievable geometry are reviewed during quotation; no universal size or tolerance is implied by the stock photograph.

Keep material and dimensional acceptance connected

Specify the grade designation, product standard, required delivery condition and material records. Agree how the blank remains traceable to the relevant parent plate. Where diameter, centre position or face condition is important, include the inspection basis on the released drawing. A material certificate alone cannot establish that a cut blank has the correct geometry.

Define dispatch condition and piece counts

List quantities by size and part mark. Identify the supplied cleaning, deburring and protection scope, along with any handling-related feature that requires separate design approval. Packing should keep thin discs or rings in their agreed delivered condition and preserve readable identity. Send the intended downstream operation with the inquiry so the review covers the actual use of the blank.
