ASTM A606 Expanded Screen Blanks

Drawing-led ASTM A606 sheet blanks proposed for expanded architectural screens, with mesh trial, edge frame and material records agreed before order.

Start with the screen duty

An expanded screen is cut and stretched from a sheet; its openings are not drilled holes. For an A606-based inquiry, identify the approved type and edition, original sheet thickness, panel size and the architectural or equipment duty. The designer should state any required loads, clearances and attachment method. Expansion changes geometry, so a flat-sheet certificate alone does not establish the performance of the finished mesh.

Define the mesh before requesting a price

Specify the long-way and short-way opening directions, strand width, open-area target and whether the mesh is flattened after expansion. Show which edge is the visible datum. A sample panel is useful for confirming the actual aperture, burr condition, stiffness and appearance before quantity production.

Detail the perimeter

Provide a drawing of the frame, unexpanded border or fixing rail. State whether the mesh is delivered as a blank or as a framed assembly. The screen engineer should approve support spacing, restraint and any need to prevent water from standing at the lower edge.

What to send

  • Approved A606 type, edition and starting sheet thickness
  • Mesh orientation, aperture and strand dimensions
  • Panel schedule, border detail and fixing drawing
  • Sample and inspection requirements
