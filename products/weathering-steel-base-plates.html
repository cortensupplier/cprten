Weathering Steel Base Plates

Weathering steel base plate blanks and processed plates supplied to an approved foundation or support-interface drawing.

SHANDONG DAZHI28 October 2026

Product scope

Base plates connect a fabricated component to its specified support interface. Quote the plate as a controlled part with an accepted anchor pattern and material requirement. The plate supplier does not establish foundation capacity or anchorage suitability from a nominal plate size.

Use one anchor and plate datum

Provide the plate outline, thickness, anchor-hole pattern and viewing direction on an approved drawing. Identify any slots, machining, edge preparation or access openings. Use a common datum with the anchor template and supporting works. A mirrored anchor pattern can preserve overall dimensions while preventing the intended installation.

Define the supplied processing stage

State whether the order covers rough blanks, cut and drilled plates, machined plates or an assembled base with attached components. Keep remaining machining allowance distinct from the finished dimensions. If welding or additional parts are included, provide their drawings and acceptance requirements. Do not assume anchors, templates or installation hardware are included unless the quotation names them.

Coordinate the concrete interface and records

The responsible designer specifies the base, anchorage, supports and interface treatment. Share any approved site measurements before fabrication release. Keep the plate mark, material identity and dimensional records linked so the receiving team can verify the correct component. Review drainage, concealed surfaces and access through the project installation detail rather than treating a grade name as a complete base design.
