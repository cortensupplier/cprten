DRAWING-LED PRODUCT INQUIRY

Weathering Steel Equipment Enclosures

Project-specific weathering steel enclosure panels and access doors, specified around equipment ventilation, maintenance and site support details.

Define the product and its interfaces

An enclosure needs an equipment clearance drawing before its steel panel schedule can be set. Intake, exhaust, door swing, service access and pipe or cable entries should come from the equipment and project designers.

Separate fabrication from project design

The metalwork quote should identify panels, frame, doors, louvres, locks and fixings separately. Electrical, fire, weather sealing and structural performance remain subject to approved project requirements; no standard enclosure rating is claimed.

What to send for a quotation

  • Equipment service envelope
  • Panel, door and louvre schedule
  • Support and anchor drawings
  • Drainage, finish and maintenance access
Material grade, achievable size, processing, availability and lead time are confirmed only after the drawing and project requirements are reviewed. This page does not claim live stock or a certified performance rating.
