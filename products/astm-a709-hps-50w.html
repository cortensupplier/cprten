ASTM A709 HPS 50W plate overview

HPS 50W is identified separately from conventional A709 Grade 50W in bridge material schedules.

Send the bridge specification, fracture-critical status where applicable, toughness requirements and approved fabrication procedure.

A quotation for ordinary A709 Grade 50W does not establish compliance with an HPS 50W requirement.

Prepare the ASTM A709 HPS 50W plate inquiry

Require the complete HPS 50W designation on the offer and mill documents, with heat traceability through cutting.

  • Full designation and governing standard edition
  • Size schedule with quantity for every plate
  • Required surface condition and treatment, if any
  • Test reports, inspection scope and heat traceability
  • Delivery destination, packing basis and required date

No fixed stock range, mill origin, processing capability or lead time is claimed here. These details are checked against the inquiry and stated in the quotation. An unpainted weathering plate also needs exposure and drainage details suited to the project.

Buyer questions

Can another weathering grade be supplied instead?

A quotation for ordinary A709 Grade 50W does not establish compliance with an HPS 50W requirement. Any substitution must be reviewed against the complete design and purchase requirements.

Does the image certify this grade?

No. The photograph illustrates flat steel plate form. Use the agreed purchase order and traceable mill records to verify the delivered grade.

How do I request a quote?
