ASTM A588 Machined Mounting Plates

A588 mounting plates quoted from finished datums, hole positions, mating faces and documented heat identity.

Material and order basis

Provide the A588 grade, standard edition, rough blank size and final machining drawing. Identify the mounting face and any critical datum, because nominal mill plate thickness alone may not define the installed fit.

Processing and acceptance

Separate machining, drilling, surface treatment and hardware supply in the quote. The structural designer controls load path and anchor design. A machined steel plate is not a certified connection without the approved assembly specification.

Identification and delivery

Mark every finished part with its drawing revision and heat reference. Protect machined faces in transit and supply an inspection record for the dimensions that control installation.

Include in your inquiry

  • Approved mounting drawing
  • Rough and finished dimensions
  • Machined face and hole datums
  • Inspection and identification records
Availability, processing capacity, size, price and lead time are confirmed against an approved purchase specification. This page does not claim live stock or a certified performance rating.
