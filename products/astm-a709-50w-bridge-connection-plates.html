Identify the bridge material requirement

State the ordered standard edition, Grade 50W designation, thickness, delivered condition and any additional project requirements. Keep Grade 50W distinct from HPS grade designations. If a different material is proposed, obtain review against the complete bridge specification before accepting it. Similar nominal strength does not by itself establish the inspection and supply basis for the project.

Supply the approved connection package

Provide plate outlines, hole sizes and coordinates, slot direction where applicable, viewing faces and mating-member references. The bridge designer controls the connection detail and its performance requirements. Ask the fabricator to confirm the permitted hole-production and edge-processing route. Identify whether plates are interchangeable or matched to specific joints and whether a defined fit check is included.

Define inspection before processing

Include the applicable toughness requirements, additional examinations, witness stages and acceptance records specified by the project. Identify which requirements apply to the relevant components and material lines. Confirm the proposed producer documentation before cutting. A general material certificate, a third-party visit and a project-specific test report are different items and should be named clearly in the agreed inspection scope.

Deliver identifiable joint sets

Preserve the heat-to-part relationship through cutting, drilling and any later operation. List the joint mark, piece marks, quantities and supplied accessories on the packing record. Separate fastener or installation scope from plate fabrication unless explicitly included. Retain the accepted drawing revision and dimensional evidence with the shipment so the receiving inspector can connect each plate to its intended joint.

Product questions

Can A588 plate automatically replace A709 Grade 50W?

Review a proposed substitution against the bridge project requirements and obtain the required approval. A similar strength description does not settle every testing and acceptance condition.

Are bolts and installation included?

Define each inclusion in the quotation. The plate fabrication scope alone does not establish fastener supply, tightening requirements or installation responsibility.
