Weathering steel for landscape projects

Weathering steel plate can be shaped into site elements that pair with planting, paving and outdoor structures. Drawing-led inquiries may include garden edging, planter boxes, raised beds, tree surrounds, low feature walls and custom landscape details.

Specify grade and thickness, dimensions, joining details, drainage, edge finish and installation method with the project design before ordering.

Specify the landscape element

Design detailInclude in the inquiry
Product typeEdging, planter, raised bed, tree surround or custom part
DimensionsLength, height, profile, corner details and quantity
MaterialGrade, product standard, thickness and supply condition
FabricationCut profiles, holes, bends, joints and assembly scope
Site conditionsSoil contact, drainage, irrigation, runoff and adjoining finishes
FinishMill finish or separately specified and approved surface treatment

Plan the quotation

Provide a drawing or dimensioned sketch and identify where each part will be installed. For planters and raised beds, include internal dimensions, base or open-bottom details, drainage provisions and intended soil depth.

  • Drawing revision and a schedule of dimensions and quantities
  • Grade, thickness and required inspection documentation
  • Connection, fixing, edge and visible-face details
  • Drainage and runoff management at the installation site
  • Packaging, destination and requested delivery schedule

Manufacturing method, dimensional limits, availability and lead time are confirmed after drawing review. Final design and structural suitability remain project-specific.
