ASTM A606 Type 5 Sheet and Strip

Prepare an A606 Type 5 sheet or strip inquiry with a confirmed standard edition, producer documentation and coil or cut-length requirements.

SHANDONG DAZHIUpdated 27 November 2026

Photograph showing sheet or coil form. Confirm the supplied type and material identity from the agreed inspection records.

ASTM A606 Type 5 is a separate type within the sheet-and-strip specification. Order it by its complete designation and the required product form rather than treating it as an interchangeable name for Type 4. The proposed producer, material records and dimensions require confirmation for the actual order.

Use a type-specific technical requirement

Provide the accepted A606/A606M edition and identify Type 5 on each relevant material line. Ask for the proposed product data and agreed inspection record for that type. Review chemistry and mechanical results against the applicable requirements and supply condition. Do not assume that an older general catalogue listing, or a Type 4 sample report, establishes the basis of a Type 5 offer.

Define the coil interface where applicable

For coil or slit-strip inquiries, state thickness, width, required quantity and the receiving line limits. Include the permitted inside diameter, outside diameter, coil mass and edge condition as applicable to the receiving equipment. Have the responsible receiving parties confirm those constraints. A total tonnage request alone does not establish that the proposed coils fit the intended processing route.

Describe cut lengths and downstream work separately

If sheets rather than coils are required, provide the finished size list and ask for the proposed cutting and levelling scope. Identify any further forming, welding or surface treatment through the project documents. Keep supplied blank dimensions distinct from final component geometry. Any proposed difference in type, product form or condition should be reviewed before it becomes part of the accepted quotation.

Keep the offered evidence with the order

Identify material and coil or bundle marks on the packing list and retain their connection to the producer record. State additional inspections explicitly rather than assuming that a generic certificate includes every project test. Review atmospheric suitability and surface expectations for the intended application. Availability, pricing and lead time follow the confirmed material and processing proposal, not the photograph used to illustrate a coil.

Product questions

Is Type 5 automatically a higher-strength Type 4?

The type names are not a simple performance ranking. Compare the ordered requirements and actual proposed product evidence before accepting any substitution.

Can a generic A606 certificate cover the inquiry?

The accepted records must identify the material and requirements relevant to the ordered type, product form and condition. Confirm the document package before placing the order.
