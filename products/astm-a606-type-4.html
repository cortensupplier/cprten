ASTM A606 Type 4 Weathering Steel Sheet

Specify A606 Type 4 weathering sheet by product form, finish, size list and exposure requirements for a project-specific quotation.

SHANDONG DAZHIUpdated 27 November 2026

Photograph showing sheet or coil form. Confirm the supplied type and material identity from the agreed inspection records.

ASTM A606 Type 4 sheet inquiries should identify both the material specification and the delivered sheet condition. Type 4 includes alloying requirements associated with improved atmospheric corrosion resistance. Its suitability for an exposed component also depends on the project environment and detail, not the grade name alone.

Prepare a usable sheet size list

Give thickness, width, cut length and piece quantity for each line, with the accepted units and dimensional requirements. Distinguish a finished panel size from a blank that will be trimmed or formed after delivery. Where a coil route is proposed, ask how cut-to-length processing and its inspection are included. Confirm that the requested form and thickness are covered by the ordered specification.

Agree the starting surface

Name the required mill condition, cleaning or other preparation and identify the visible face where it matters. If an initial weathered finish is requested, agree a representative sample and a defined review stage. A delivery photograph taken under different lighting or wetness is not a reliable colour acceptance reference. Include any agreed treatment as a named part of the supply scope.

Review the intended exposure

Type 4 may be used bare in suitable atmospheric applications, subject to the project requirements and exposure assessment. Provide information about wetting and drying, salt exposure, retained moisture and runoff interfaces. Have the responsible project team assess drainage and adjoining materials. Do not replace that review with a general promise that every rust layer becomes protective or that one colour will remain fixed.

Release processing and records together

For shaped or folded components, send the approved drawing, bend references, cutouts and final edge requirements. Ask the processor to confirm the proposed material and fabrication route before work is released. Keep the Type 4 material identification connected to the supplied pieces and specify any inspection reports needed. The quotation can then distinguish stock sheet, prepared blanks and further fabricated components.

Product questions

Is A606 Type 4 a generic designation for heavy plate?

A606 covers defined sheet-and-strip forms. The ordered thickness and whether material is supplied in coils or cut lengths must be checked against the specification scope.

Does pre-weathering fix the final colour?

State the accepted starting appearance and review conditions. Later appearance remains affected by exposure, so the sample agreement should describe what it represents.

Compare A606 type requirements

Keep the specified type explicit when reviewing Type 2 sheet and strip, Type 4 weathering sheet and Type 5 sheet and strip.
