Material overview

A606 applies to sheet and strip, with scope depending on the rolled form and ordered thickness. Type 4 is a material requirement; it should be stated together with form, thickness, condition and the applicable edition.

Do not use A606 as a generic heavy-plate specification. Confirm that the requested thickness and delivered form are inside the standard’s scope.

Prepare the order details

Identify the required dimensions in millimeters, quantity in pieces and any tolerances beyond the agreed product standard. For shaped parts, include the drawing revision, cutting allowance and visible-face requirements.

  • Thickness × width × length, with quantities for every size
  • Mill finish, natural weathering or a separately agreed treatment
  • Delivered condition, identification and inspection records
  • Site exposure, intended application and drainage considerations
  • Destination, delivery basis, packing and requested schedule

Processing methods, achievable sizes, sample approval and lead time are reviewed against the actual requirement. No fixed stock or capacity is implied by this page.
