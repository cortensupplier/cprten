ASTM A606 Type 2 is a sheet-and-strip material designation for a purchase specification requiring its particular chemistry and mechanical-property basis. Send the complete standard edition, Type 2 designation, rolling condition and required product form. Availability, dimensions and document scope are confirmed for the actual inquiry.

Identify Type 2 explicitly

The A606/A606M-25 scope identifies Type 2 as containing at least 0.20 percent copper on cast or heat analysis, with 0.18 percent minimum for product check. Those figures concern one element and its analysis basis; they are not a complete acceptance table. Compare the full ordered requirements with the proposed producer records rather than identifying the material from copper content or surface colour alone.

Order sheet or strip in the required condition

State whether the inquiry concerns cut lengths or a coil and whether hot-rolled or cold-rolled supply is required. Give thickness, width, length where applicable and quantity. The standard treats product form and thickness together; do not assume that every heavy cut plate is within A606 scope. If the requested dimensions need a different material route, resolve it before quotation acceptance.

Define edges, surface and later processing

Specify the supplied edge condition and any trimming, cutting or levelling required. Explain the next fabrication operation so the proposed material and delivered condition can be reviewed for that task. Keep paint, cleaning, pre-weathering and other treatments as explicit requirements. Type 2 should not be substituted for Type 4 or Type 5 solely to obtain a similar appearance.

Connect the documents to delivered material

Request the agreed inspection document and maintain its connection to the heat and supplied bundle or coil. Where separate product analysis is required, define sampling and assessment through the purchase requirements. The quotation should identify included processing, packaging and inspection services. Send the destination and required schedule with the material list so the offer can address the complete supply scope.

Product questions

Does Type 2 mean the same as Type 4?

No. Keep the specified type and its full requirements on the purchase line; a type change needs acceptance against the project specification.

Can I identify Type 2 from a rusted photograph?

Use the material records and heat identification. An image describes appearance or product form, not the chemical and mechanical identity of a delivery.
