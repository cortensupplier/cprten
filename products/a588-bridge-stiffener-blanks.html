A588 PLATE PRODUCT INQUIRY

ASTM A588 Bridge Stiffener Blanks

Drawing-led A588 stiffener blanks for approved bridge fabrication packages, with cut geometry and identification controlled by location.

Material and purchase basis

A bridge stiffener schedule should list each location, parent member, nominal plate thickness and finished profile. The bridge engineer must approve the material and detail; an A588 inquiry cannot silently replace an A709 requirement.

Define the processing

Show corners, weld access cuts, chamfers and any orientation that affects fit. Ask the fabricator to confirm cutting sequence and allowances before the final blanks are released.

Identification and handover

Pack by girder or assembly mark and keep heat number references with the pieces. If inspection or witness points are required, name them on the purchase schedule before cutting starts.

Send these details

  • Engineer-approved material callout
  • Girder and location schedule
  • Profile and weld-access detail
  • Heat identity and inspection plan
Availability, processing capacity, size, price and lead time are confirmed after review of the approved specification and drawing. This page does not claim live stock or a certified performance rating.
