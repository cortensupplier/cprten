ASTM A588 Structural Cover Plates

A588 cover plates for engineer-approved strengthening or connection details, scoped by fit, holes, welds and existing-member survey.

Material and application

Identify the specified A588 grade, edition, thickness and the member receiving each cover plate. The structural engineer defines capacity and whether the detail is a repair, reinforcement or new connection.

Processing and acceptance

Send an as-built survey when plates fit existing steelwork. Define trimmed edges, hole pattern, weld preparation and any curvature separately. A nominal rectangular plate description cannot establish the required fit against an installed member.

Identification and delivery

Mark each piece by location and drawing revision. Include the heat certificate mapping and inspection record requested by the project, with any approved site adjustments documented before dispatch.

Include in your inquiry

  • Engineer-approved detail
  • Existing-member measurements
  • Hole and weld preparation scope
  • Location and heat identification
Grade or type, dimensions, processing capacity, availability, price and lead time are confirmed after the approved documents are reviewed. This page does not claim live stock or a certified performance rating.
