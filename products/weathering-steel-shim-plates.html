DRAWING-DEFINED PLATE COMPONENTS

Weathering Steel Shim Plates

Solid and slotted plate shims made to an approved footprint and thickness schedule for project-specific interfaces.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Steel plate stock shown for material context. See the geometry schematic and approved project drawing for the component outline.

Request shim plates as individually identified spacers or as a scheduled pack of pieces. Specify the material and interface requirements through the project design. Weathering steel should not be selected solely to match the name of an adjoining component, particularly where the shim sits in a concealed or moisture-retaining position.

Define the footprint and installation route

Provide a drawing for the solid, split or slotted profile. Include holes, opening direction, contact area and any approved retention feature. Explain whether the piece is inserted before assembly or introduced around an existing fixing. This distinction can change the required outline even when the nominal adjustment thickness is the same.

List thicknesses by piece

For a pack, give the thickness and count of each component along with its intended position. State the permitted variation and whether precision machining is required. A plate cut to outline is not automatically a ground or precision-machined spacer. Availability of the requested thicknesses and the proposed processing route must be confirmed in the offer.

Agree seating surfaces and edge finish

Identify the accepted contact-face condition, burr removal and any requirement for rounded or treated edges. The project designer defines the support and interface arrangement, including the permitted stack configuration and protection. Do not assume that loose spare pieces can be added at installation without reviewing the approved build-up and resulting fit.

Label packs for receiving and installation

Use pack marks and location references on the contents list. State whether pieces are delivered loose or held together by an agreed transport arrangement. Keep temporary packaging separate from permanent retention. Supply the required material and dimensional records, and record any approved changes to the pack combination so replacement pieces can be ordered from the accepted installed arrangement.
