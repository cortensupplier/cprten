DRAWING-DEFINED PLATE COMPONENTS

Weathering Steel Splice Plates

Drawing-defined plates for approved splice connections, supplied with the specified outline, hole pattern and set identification.

SHANDONG DAZHI29 October 2026

Steel plate stock shown for material context. See the geometry schematic and approved project drawing for the component outline.

Request splice plates against an approved connection drawing and a component schedule. The supply can cover agreed cut blanks or plates with specified drilled or cut features, subject to fabrication review. Connection design, material suitability and the fastening arrangement must be established in the project documents before production release.

Provide the joint and loose-part drawings

Show plate thickness, outline, hole sizes, hole coordinates and viewing direction. Include the mating-member references so the fabricator can understand which pieces form a set. Where opposite-side plates differ, identify their hands separately. Do not rely on a generic connection picture to define the required spacing, edge distances or plate capacity.

Agree the hole-production and fit requirements

State the required hole condition, inspection scope and any restrictions from the project specification. Ask the processor to confirm the proposed route for the actual material and geometry. Define whether supplied pieces are interchangeable or matched to particular members, and whether a template check or trial assembly is included. Any change affecting the connection requires the accepted project approval route.

Specify contact and exposed surfaces

Distinguish the joint contact faces from surfaces exposed to view. The connection designer determines any relevant surface preparation or protection requirement. Keep that requirement in the purchase scope rather than assuming that an outdoor weathering finish is also suitable for every concealed interface. Any cleaning, edge finishing or additional treatment should be named in the quotation.

Preserve set identity at delivery

List the joint reference, plate mark, quantity and supplied accessories for each set. Retain material traceability and the agreed dimensional records. Mark or label the pieces using an accepted method and package incomplete sets visibly if split delivery has been agreed. Fasteners, installation and structural verification are included only when specifically stated in the accepted supply scope.
