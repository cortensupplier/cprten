ASTM A588 Girder Web and Flange Blanks

ASTM A588 plate blanks for engineer-designed web and flange fabrication, scheduled by grade, thickness, weld preparation and heat identity.

Separate the web and flange schedule

Fabricated girders may use different plate thicknesses and widths for webs, top flanges and bottom flanges. List each blank by member mark, position, length, width, thickness and quantity. Name the exact ASTM A588 grade and edition on the purchase specification rather than relying on the general term “weathering plate.”

Control interfaces before cutting

The approved girder drawing must locate splices, stiffeners, camber references, end cuts and any openings. State which edges receive weld preparation and which remain as-cut. If a flange requires a deliberate allowance for later trimming or machining, show it on the cut list instead of letting the supplier infer it.

Keep plate identity through fabrication

Define the material certificate, heat marking and transfer-mark method expected after nesting. A member assembled from several heats needs a record that connects each web and flange blank to the finished girder mark. The fabricator and engineer retain responsibility for welding procedure, inspection and structural acceptance.

Inquiry package

  • Approved member schedule and plate grade
  • Blank dimensions and edge preparation
  • Required records, tests and identification marks
  • Packing by girder and fabrication sequence
