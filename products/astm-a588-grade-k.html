Grade K, carried through the order

ASTM A588 Grade K is a grade-specific structural weathering steel inquiry option. The full grade belongs in the material schedule, quotation and purchase order, with the standard edition and delivered form agreed before supply. Grade K should not be assumed to replace Grade A or Grade B without an approved review.

We review requests for plate dimensions, quantities, documentation and processing against the submitted specification. Available sizes, material origin, inspection scope and lead time are confirmed in the quotation.

A grade name is not a universal thickness or strength guarantee. Check acceptance values against the applicable standard table, ordered thickness and project requirements.

Structural fabrication and exposure

A588 material is used in structural fabrication where the approved design calls for atmospheric corrosion resistance. Welding, edge preparation and inspection should follow the project’s accepted procedures. If bridge work has additional requirements, review the governing bridge specification instead of assuming an A588 description covers every obligation.

For bare exposure, discuss drying, drainage, salt contamination, concealed joints and runoff. A natural patina does not remove the need for detailing and site assessment. An initial surface sample and material certification serve different purposes.

Is Grade K automatically a higher grade than A or B?

No. The letters identify distinct grade requirements. Select the designation required by the approved specification.

Can a photograph confirm ASTM A588 Grade K?

No. Material identity is established through the agreed inspection records and traceable identification.

Are stock sizes and delivery times fixed?

No. Confirm availability, achievable dimensions, processing and schedule in a project quotation.
