UNDERSTAND THE PATINA

More than
a rust-colored finish.

Weathering steel is specified for its material properties, not simply its appearance. Under suitable wet and dry exposure, a protective oxide layer develops over time.

Good design gives that surface the conditions it needs: drainage, ventilation and careful consideration of the surrounding environment.

  • Specify the actual grade and standard
  • Consider exposure and water runoff
  • Agree finish samples and acceptance details

All questions ↗
Does weathering steel stop rusting?

No. In suitable exposure, a protective patina develops and slows further corrosion. The environment, detailing and inspection remain important throughout service.

Is COR-TEN the same as any weathering steel?

COR-TEN is a trademark. Use the actual grade and standard in your order. A generic weathering-steel inquiry does not establish licensed COR-TEN origin.

Can I order a specific rust color?

Use an agreed physical sample for initial appearance. Natural weathering varies with exposure, orientation and time; an exact permanent color should not be assumed.

Can I use it near the sea?

Marine salts, deicing salts and persistent dampness need a project-specific assessment. Provide the site location and exposure details before material selection.
