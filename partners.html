Tell us what your customers specify

Distributor inquiries are most useful when they identify the common grades, product forms, size ranges and documents requested in the destination market. Share the standards your customers use and distinguish planned stock material from project-specific purchasing.

Build a focused inquiry package

  • Company introduction and destination market
  • Typical material grades, sizes and purchase quantities
  • Required documents and receiving standards
  • Cut-size, finish, labeling and packing requirements
  • Expected purchase schedule and delivery basis

Agree scope before making commitments

Discuss availability, batch requirements, quotation validity and processing options for the actual material list. Commercial terms, branding arrangements, territory rights and any exclusivity require a separate agreement; submitting an inquiry does not create a partnership appointment.

Start with the material list

Email cortensupplier@gmail.com with your introduction and buying requirements, or use our RFQ form to prepare the first material inquiry. Relevant company documents and confirmed supply information can be requested during qualification.
