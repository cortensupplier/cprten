PLATE FAQ

Questions before ordering weathering steel plate.

Does a rust-coloured surface prove the grade?

No. Verify the grade from the agreed certificate and heat identification, not appearance.

Which size should I request?

Send required thickness, width, length, quantity and tolerances. If cut blanks are needed, include the approved drawing and allowances.

Can one grade replace another?

Only after the purchaser and responsible engineer approve the substitution against the project requirements.

What about outdoor exposure?

Drainage, wetting and drying, salt exposure and runoff can affect performance and appearance. The project designer should review the site conditions.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the plate RFQ form or the downloadable template.
