LANDSCAPE DESIGN

Landscape & gardens

Give outdoor spaces a distinctive material edge.

Start with the project details.

Planters, edging and screens combine appearance with practical exposure details. Persistent dampness, trapped soil, irrigation and contact with paving can create conditions very different from an open vertical panel. Describe the actual use and agree the lining, drainage and support details with the project designer.

Questions to resolve

  • Soil, irrigation and prolonged moisture exposure
  • Drainage and separation from moisture-retaining materials
  • Edge treatment and public-contact surfaces
  • Panel joins, support and finish-sample requirements

Material and component inquiries

Planter material, landscape edging blanks and screen-panel inquiries.

Share the approved grade, drawings, dimensions and required documentation. Material selection, structural design and installation details must be coordinated by the responsible project team. Processing and supply scope are confirmed in the quotation.
