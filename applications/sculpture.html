PUBLIC SPACES

Sculpture & public art

Let material and form evolve together.

Start with the project details.

Weathering steel can bring a changing surface to sculpture and public installations. The fabrication package still needs clear material identity, connection details and an approved design. Discuss visual expectations alongside handling, transport, public access and runoff onto the surrounding site.

Questions to resolve

  • Base material, plate thickness and fabrication sequence
  • Stability, foundations and connections by the designer
  • Accessible edges and public-contact surfaces
  • Expected patina variation and surrounding surfaces

Material and component inquiries

Sculpture blanks, profiled plate and public-art fabrication inquiries.

Share the approved grade, drawings, dimensions and required documentation. Material selection, structural design and installation details must be coordinated by the responsible project team. Processing and supply scope are confirmed in the quotation.
